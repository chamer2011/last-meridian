In a line:
A Hegemony border guard, with a carbine, a checklist and no imagination.

When the fight starts:
- A marine in Hegemony grey asks for your transit papers, notes for the record that you've declined, and unslings a carbine.
- A customs marine recites the article you're violating, and thumbs off the safety at the end of the sentence.

When they hit:
- A short burst hits you, and the marine calls out the time for the record.
- The carbine butt comes around and catches you under the ribs, exactly as the manual shows.
- The marine fires again, and informs you that resisting adds a further penalty.

When they miss:
- The burst goes over your head. The marine frowns and corrects their stance.
- Rounds stitch the crate beside you, and you hear the marine report the property damage.
- You duck behind a stack of impounded cargo, and the marine walks their fire along it, one item at a time.

When they hit hard:
- The marine puts a round into you from the regulation distance, and calmly advises you of your rights.

When they're beaten:
- The marine goes down, and their recorder keeps running, listing your offenses to the empty air.
- The marine drops to one knee, and asks you to sign for the damage.

When they join the fight:
- {Enemy} arrives at a regulation jog, and orders you to remain where you are.

When they're stunned:
- {Enemy} stands frozen with one hand on the checklist, and you get a moment to breathe.
