In a line:
Gilded Wheel security, with more money in their arms than you have in your ship.

When the fight starts:
- A very well-dressed person with very expensive arms asks you to step away from the table, and cracks each finger individually.
- House security arrives without any fuss. One of them takes off a dinner jacket, folds it, and looks at you.

When they hit:
- A gilded fist hits you with a piston's thump, and you can hear the servos working.
- They catch your wrist, turn it the way it doesn't go, and apologize on behalf of the House.
- An arm extends further than any arm should, and collects you from across the table.

When they miss:
- The fist punches through the card table beside you, and chips rain down around your feet.
- You slip under the swing, and the enforcer straightens a cuff as if that was the plan.
- You duck, and the blow takes a chunk out of the gilded pillar behind you. Somebody makes a note of it, and of you.

When they hit hard:
- The arm hits you, then a second charge fires inside it, and the second hit is the one you feel.

When they're beaten:
- The augments whine down, and the enforcer tells you, quite politely, that the House will add this to your bill.
- The enforcer drops, and a small gold light on their wrist starts blinking, calling someone on your behalf.

When they join the fight:
- {Enemy} appears at your elbow without seeming to have crossed the room.

When they're stunned:
- {Enemy} stops dead and resets their stance, servos ticking, while you catch your breath.
