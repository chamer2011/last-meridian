In a line:
A long, armored creature from the lava tubes, which eats heat and isn't fussy about whose.

When the fight starts:
- The wall of the lava tube moves. A long body of cooled slag drags itself out, and you feel the warmth go out of the air around you.
- Your breath suddenly fogs, in a tunnel hot enough to cook in. Something long turns toward the warmth of you.

When they hit:
- Mandibles crusted with slag close on your arm, and the cold runs right up to your shoulder.
- Its tail whips through the heavy air and knocks your legs out from under you.
- It presses its glowing underside against you, and you feel it drink the warmth from your whole side.

When they miss:
- It strikes where you were standing, and leaves a bite-shaped hole in solid rock.
- You drag yourself clear, every step heavy in this gravity, and the mandibles close behind you.
- It lunges at you and overshoots, and its segments grind past you in a shower of hot grit.

When they hit hard:
- It wraps around you, and everything warm in you starts to leave, and you've never been so cold.

When they're beaten:
- The glow fades along its seams, segment by segment, until you're looking at a long heap of slag.
- It curls up and cracks, and the heat it stole rolls back over you in one great breath.

When they join the fight:
- A seam in the rock beside you splits open, and {enemy} pours out of it, glowing.

When they're stunned:
- {Enemy} coils in on itself with its seams dimming, and loses track of you.
