In a line:
A salvager who finds it easier to rob divers than to dive.

When the fight starts:
- A patched hard suit drifts out from behind the wreck with a hull torch lit, and a voice on your comms says this claim is theirs now.
- You find a second set of mag-boot tracks in the dust on the hull, just before their owner finds you.

When they hit:
- The hull torch rakes across you, and your suit alarm starts chirping.
- They kick off the bulkhead and slam into you, and a pry bar finds the joint in your armor.
- A magnetic grapple pins your arm to the hull, and the torch comes in right behind it.

When they miss:
- The torch carves a bright line across the plate beside your head.
- They swing the pry bar at you, and the recoil sends them spinning away, cursing.
- You duck behind a spar, and the torch cuts it in half instead of you.

When they hit hard:
- The torch finds your air line, and for three long breaths you're doing sums about how much you have left.

When they're beaten:
- Their boots lose the hull, and they drift slowly away from you, still offering to split it.
- They let go of the torch and raise both hands, and ask you whether the Guild really needs to hear about this.

When they join the fight:
- {Enemy} swings in on a salvage line with a torch already lit, looking to take a share of whatever you've got.

When they're stunned:
- {Enemy} hangs off their line, blinking at you, as the torch gutters in their hand.
