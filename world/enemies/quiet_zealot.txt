In a line:
A believer in the Quiet, unarmed-looking and completely unafraid.

When the fight starts:
- A calm person with a silver disc at their throat walks toward you with open hands, and tells you that it doesn't hurt. You're fairly sure they don't mean the fight.
- A believer of the Quiet steps out of the crowd, smiling at you as if you've finally arrived.

When they hit:
- They don't flinch at all. They simply reach you, and a sharpened tool comes out of a sleeve.
- A hand closes on your collar with no hurry in it, and the blade follows. "Shh," they tell you.
- You hear humming very close to your ear, just as the pain arrives.

When they miss:
- They reach for you, and you shove them back. They nod, as if you've made a good point.
- The blade passes your ribs. They don't look disappointed, and that's what bothers you.
- You knock them down. They get up again at the same pace, still smiling at you.

When they hit hard:
- They rest their forehead against yours, gently, and the blade goes in, and they tell you that it's nearly over.

When they're beaten:
- They go down with the same calm smile, and you find that you don't want to look at it.
- They lie on the deck looking past you, and thank you.

When they join the fight:
- {Enemy} walks in without hurrying, and doesn't so much as glance at your weapon.

When they're stunned:
- {Enemy} stands with their eyes closed, humming, and doesn't seem to know that you're there.

When persuading works:
- You ask what the Quiet would say about all this noise. They stop, and they tilt their head, and they listen for a long time. When they speak, it's to say that you may go.
