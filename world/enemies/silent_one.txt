In a line:
A person who has listened to the Quiet for too long, and who no longer makes any sound.

When the fight starts:
- You notice the silence first. Then you notice that someone has been standing very close to you for some time.
- A person in fleet clothes walks toward you, and their footsteps make no sound, and neither does anything else near them, including you.

When they hit:
- They touch you, and the sound of your own heartbeat goes away, and then the pain comes in with no noise at all.
- You shout, and nothing comes out of you, and their hand is already on your chest.
- Everything goes soft and still, and you're so tired, and then you realize you've been hit.

When they miss:
- Their hand passes your face, and you feel the air go dead where it was.
- You stagger out of the silence around them, and all the noise of the world comes back at once.
- You can't hear your own feet, but you get clear, and they turn to follow you without a sound.

When they hit hard:
- The quiet closes over you completely, and it's the most restful thing you've ever felt, and you have to fight very hard to want to come back.

When they're beaten:
- They fold up without a sound, and slowly the noise of the room comes back to you.
- They lie still with their lips moving. You lean in, and there's nothing to hear.

When they join the fight:
- The noise around you starts to fade on one side, and you turn to find {enemy} standing there.

When they're stunned:
- {Enemy} stands with their head tilted, listening to something, and the quiet lets go of you.
