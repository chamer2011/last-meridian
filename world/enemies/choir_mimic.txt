In a line:
A Choir machine that wears a face you trust, until it doesn't.

When the fight starts:
- Someone you know walks toward you down the corridor and says your name, and then you notice that they haven't blinked.
- A familiar voice asks if you're all right, and you're already turning to answer when you hear the humming under it.

When they hit:
- It hits you with a hand you've shaken a hundred times, and the fingers are silver underneath.
- It goes on talking to you in that familiar voice, about something you once told them, while it hurts you.
- It catches you as if to steady you, and the grip keeps tightening.

When they miss:
- The hand stops where your throat was, and the face gives you a small, apologetic smile.
- You pull away, and it tilts its head, and tries a different expression on you.
- It swings at you and misses, and says your name, hurt, as if you'd been the one to strike first.

When they hit hard:
- The face leans close and says something only they could have known, and while you're hearing it, the silver goes in.

When they're beaten:
- The face slides and runs, and for a moment, before it's only silver, it looks at you with real surprise.
- It drops, and the voice goes on for a little while, asking you whether you're all right.

When they join the fight:
- {Enemy} comes in behind you wearing a face you'd have trusted, and you wish you hadn't looked.

When they're stunned:
- {Enemy} flickers through three faces, all of them looking at you, and can't settle on one.
