# Syndicate Collector
threat: 1
hp: 15
attack: 3
defense: 12
damage: 2 to 5
weapon: Sleeve Pistol
xp: 25
credits: 20 to 70
art: scoundrel
tags: syndicate
motive: paid
faction: drift

In a line:
A Syndicate debt collector, polite for about the first minute.

When the fight starts:
- A neat person with a data slate asks whether you have a moment to discuss the balance outstanding. Their other hand is already inside their coat.
- A collector in a very good coat reads you a sum, with interest, and seems truly sorry when you don't reach for your wallet.

When they hit:
- A small pistol comes out of a sleeve and fires twice at you, and the collector makes a note on the slate.
- The slate cracks you across the jaw. "Late fee," the collector says.
- The collector steps in close, straightens your collar, and puts the pistol against your ribs.

When they miss:
- The sleeve pistol spits past your ear. "That was your first reminder," the collector says.
- You knock the pistol aside, and the collector sighs and adjusts their cuffs.
- The collector fires, misses you, and apologizes for the inconvenience.

When they hit hard:
- The collector shoots you precisely where you'll still be able to sign.

When they're beaten:
- The collector sits down, takes out the slate with a shaking hand, and marks your account as overdue.
- The collector folds, and the slate skitters to your feet, showing a very long column of figures.

When they join the fight:
- {Enemy} arrives straightening their cuffs, and adds your name to the slate.

When they're stunned:
- {Enemy} pauses with one finger on the slate, trying to remember what they were about to charge you.

When persuading works:
- You ask to see the paperwork, and you find a mistake in it. The collector sighs, closes the little book, and says they'll be in touch.

When paying works:
- The collector counts it, makes a note in a little book, and thanks you for your business. There's even a receipt.
