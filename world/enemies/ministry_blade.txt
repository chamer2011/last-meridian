In a line:
A secret-police duelist from Vantor, trained from childhood and unfailingly polite.

When the fight starts:
- A slim figure in Ministry grey offers you a small bow, and then a choice of where you'd like to be wounded first.
- A Ministry Blade removes one glove, salutes you with a thin saber, and asks you to please begin.

When they hit:
- You don't see the saber move. You only see the neat line it left across your sleeve.
- The blade flicks past your guard and back. "First lesson," says the duelist, not even breathing hard.
- A thin cut opens across your knuckles, and the duelist corrects your grip with the flat of the blade.

When they miss:
- The saber stops a finger's width from your eye, and the duelist tuts at themselves.
- You parry more by luck than anything, and the duelist raises an eyebrow at you, pleased.
- You stumble back out of reach, and the duelist waits for you to find your feet, as good manners require.

When they hit hard:
- It's a beautiful piece of swordwork, and you're still admiring it when your knees give.

When they're beaten:
- The duelist lowers the saber, touches their own wound, and looks at you as if you've said something rude.
- The duelist falls in good order, and from the floor asks you to note the time.

When they join the fight:
- {Enemy} arrives without hurrying, and waits for a gap in the fighting to bow to you.

When they're stunned:
- {Enemy} takes one careful step back and holds their guard, blinking, and doesn't come at you.

When persuading fails:
- The blade agrees with everything you say, thanks you for saying it, and doesn't stop.

When a lie works:
- You give a Ministry countersign you once overheard, in the right order. The blade bows, apologizes for the misunderstanding, and leaves without a sound.
