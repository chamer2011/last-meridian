In a line:
A Concord marine who never stopped following orders, in armor kept like new.

When the fight starts:
- A marine in spotless Concord plate challenges you by the book, asks for your authorization, and doesn't wait long for it.
- A Concord marine gives you the last lawful warning. You haven't heard that phrase since the Fall.

When they hit:
- Two rounds, one breath, and a correction. You'd admire it if it weren't aimed at you.
- The marine advances by the manual and puts a burst through your cover, and you recognize the drill.
- A rifle butt snaps out, short and exact, and puts you on your back.

When they miss:
- Rounds snap past your head in a tight group, just one hand's width too high.
- The marine shifts to the next position on the count, which gives you one second to get clear.
- You throw yourself behind a bulkhead, and the burst walks along it and stops exactly at the edge.

When they hit hard:
- You know this one. It's the shot from the recruiting posters, and you've just been hit by it.

When they're beaten:
- The marine goes down on one knee, and asks you for your name and rank, for the report.
- The marine sets the rifle down, safety on, before falling. You've never seen anyone do that.

When they join the fight:
- {Enemy} falls in from behind cover, calling out your position to someone you can't see.

When they're stunned:
- {Enemy} holds position with a blank visor, waiting for an order that isn't coming, and you have a moment.
