Whatever has been living in the flooded levels of Maris Deep.

When the fight starts:
- Something climbs out of the flooded stairwell behind you, streaming black water, with a station ID badge tangled in what it has for a chest.
- You hear wet footsteps keeping time with yours. They stop when you stop.

When they hit:
- A cold, wet weight wraps around your arm and starts pulling you toward the water.
- It hits you with something that bends in too many places, and the cold soaks into you.
- Black water fills your mouth from nowhere, and for a moment you're at the bottom of the sea.

When they miss:
- It reaches for you and closes on nothing, and the water running off it hisses on the deck.
- You scramble back from the edge as it surges up, and it slides under again without a ripple.
- It comes at you in the dark, and misses, and you hear it breathing water right behind you.

When they hit hard:
- It gets you under, only for a few seconds, and down there in the dark you hear a calm voice reading out numbers.

When they're beaten:
- It slumps apart into a spreading pool around your boots, and the badge is left lying in the wet.
- It sinks back into the flooded stairwell, the water goes flat and still, and you keep your eyes on it as you back away.

When they join the fight:
- The water behind you bulges, and {enemy} hauls itself up out of it.

When they're stunned:
- {Enemy} sways where it stands, dripping, as if it has forgotten what you are.
