HOW TO WRITE AN ENEMY
=====================

Every kind of enemy the player can fight is one file in this folder, named by its id: dock_thug.txt is the Dock
Thug, and places and stories name enemies by that id. The Story board in the game (The story, then Story board,
then Enemies) shows and changes the same files, and npm run check-story reads them all and says what's wrong, with
the file and the line. People with a file of their own in world/characters fight from that file instead.

    # Dock Thug
    threat: 1                           (1 to 5: how dangerous, and how the game scales them)
    hp: 14
    attack: 2
    defense: 11
    damage: 2 to 5
    weapon: Stun Baton
    xp: 20                              (what beating one is worth)
    credits: 10 to 40                   (what they carry)
    art: brawler                        (how they're drawn: brawler, soldier, trooper, drone, synthetic, hunter, ...)
    tags: machines, choir               (what they count as, for conditions and perks; leave it empty if none)
    motive: paid                        (why they fight: paid, greed, orders, belief, cause, honor, hunger, desperate,
                                         guarding, programmed or choir; the parley works from it)
    faction: drift                      (who they fight for, by id; leave it out if nobody)
    machine: yes                        (leave it out if not)
    boss: yes                           (leave it out if not)
    on hit: stress 2                    (what a hit also does to the player: stress or energy; leave it out if nothing)

    In a line:
    Hired muscle with a stun baton.

    When the fight starts:
    - A thug with a stun baton steps out from between the cargo stacks.
    When they hit:
    - The baton catches you across the ribs.
    When they miss:
    - The baton whistles past your ear.
    When they hit hard:
    - ...
    When they're beaten:
    - ...
    When they join the fight:
    - ...
    When they're stunned:
    - ...
    When persuading works:              (and When persuading fails, When paying works, When a threat works,
    - ...                                When a lie works, When calming works, When a shutdown works)

The lines are read out in a fight, one picked at random each time; a kind of line that's left out gets a plain
one. A part that has nothing in it can be left out.
