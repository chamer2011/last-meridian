In a line:
A tall Choir singer that coordinates the others, and everything nearby moves to its song.

When the fight starts:
- A tall silver shape unfolds at the end of the hall with its throat open, and you feel the note it's holding in the fluid of your eyes.
- You hear the hum change key around you, and everything silver in the room starts to move in time. Then you see what's conducting it.

When they hit:
- It changes key, and something in your chest changes with it, and you drop to one knee.
- A spear of sound crosses the room and goes through you. It's a beautiful note, and that's the worst of it.
- You hear the harmony shift just before the blow, and you understand that it's singing about you now.

When they miss:
- The note passes over you, and the bulkhead behind you starts to ring, and then to crack.
- It holds the note and turns slowly, and the sound slides past you, searching.
- You clap your hands over your ears and stumble clear, and the note bends after you, too late.

When they hit hard:
- The song stops for one beat, and everything it's aimed at stops too, which right now is you.

When they're beaten:
- Its throat closes on a note that never ends, and you're still hearing it after it falls.
- It falls mid-phrase. Somewhere very far away, you hear the song pick up the missing part.

When they join the fight:
- You hear {enemy} before you see it, and your hands start keeping time against your will.

When they're stunned:
- {Enemy} stops on a held breath with its throat working, and for one moment you can hear yourself think.
