In a line:
The last of the Concord's best, with nothing left to lose.

When the fight starts:
- You don't hear the commando arrive. You hear the door seal behind you, and then a quiet voice telling you to put it down.
- A figure in stripped-down Concord armor, with every unit mark cut off it, steps out of the shadow you just checked.

When they hit:
- A knife comes out of nowhere and finds the gap in your guard, and the commando is already gone again.
- Three suppressed rounds hit you in the time it takes you to hear the first.
- The commando uses your own momentum, puts you into the wall, and takes a shot while you're there.

When they miss:
- You catch a flicker of movement and throw yourself sideways, and rounds chew up the place you just left.
- The knife misses you by the width of your collar, and the commando shows no reaction at all.
- You fire blind at the doorway, and the commando, who was about to come through it, decides not to.

When they hit hard:
- You realize that the first two attacks were only there to put you where you're standing now.

When they're beaten:
- The commando sags against the wall. "We held the line," they tell you. "Where were you?"
- The commando eases down the wall, and nods to you, one professional to another.

When they join the fight:
- {Enemy} is suddenly just there at your shoulder, and you never saw the door open.

When they're stunned:
- {Enemy} rocks back and takes a full breath, the first sign you've seen that they're human.
