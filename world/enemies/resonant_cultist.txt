In a line:
A heretic adept from Saint Orrin's Comet, who burns herself up as she fights.

When the fight starts:
- A gaunt woman in scorched robes walks toward you with light leaking from her hands, and tells you that the monks of Ilun have lied to you about what this costs.
- The air starts to smell of burned hair. A heretic in ash-grey robes smiles at you through cracked lips, and opens her hands.

When they hit:
- The air between you buckles, and the blow that lands on you smells of burned hair, most of it hers.
- She closes her fist, and you feel your strength go with it, and a new burn opens on her wrist.
- She lays a line of white fire across you, and laughs as if you've both been let in on something.

When they miss:
- What she throws at you goes wide and tears a plate off the wall, and she laughs through cracked lips.
- You throw yourself flat, and it hums past you, and her sleeve catches fire. She doesn't seem to notice.
- You get behind cover, and she stops to catch her breath, smoking gently, and tells you it won't help.

When they hit hard:
- She lays one burning hand on your chest, and for a moment you feel everything she's paid, all at once.

When they're beaten:
- The light gutters out of her hands. She sits down in the ash of her own sleeves, and tells you you'll understand when it's your turn.
- She looks at you with great relief, and asks you whether it's finally quiet.

- {Enemy} walks in through the smoke with her hands already alight, and smiles at you as if you were expected.

- {Enemy} doubles over with smoke rising from her sleeves, and can't do anything to you just yet.
