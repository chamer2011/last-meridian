In a line:
A silver scout of the machine fleet. It hums in harmony.

When the fight starts:
- A silver drone drifts into view, humming a note you can feel in your teeth.

When they hit:
- A lance of silver light goes through you, and the humming climbs.
- It strikes, and for an instant you hear voices you know.

When they miss:
- The lance of light cuts through the bulkhead where you stood.
- It adjusts its pitch, as if listening, and fires wide.

When they hit hard:
- The note rises until you can't think, and then the light finds you.

When they're beaten:
- The humming falls silent, and the drone drops, ringing, to the deck.
