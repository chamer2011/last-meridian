# Desperate Refugee

In a line:
Someone from the fleet who has run out of options, and knows it.

When the fight starts:
- A thin figure in a fleet-issue blanket steps into your way, holding a kitchen knife in both hands. They tell you they're sorry before you've said anything.
- Someone pushes out of the crowd and grabs at what you're carrying. When you turn, you see a knife, and a face that hasn't eaten in days.

When they hit:
- The knife catches your arm, and they look more frightened by it than you are.
- They swing with their eyes shut, and it still hurts you.
- They grab your coat and hang on with surprising strength, and the knife comes around at you again.

When they miss:
- The knife goes wide of you, and the swing nearly takes them off their feet.
- You step aside, and they stumble past you, gasping with the effort.
- They lunge at you, stop, and look at the knife as if someone else had put it there.

When they hit hard:
- They throw their whole weight at you, everything they have left, and the knife finds you before either of you understands what's happened.

When they're beaten:
- They drop the knife and sit down on the deck, and you can see how thin their wrists are.
- They put both hands up, and ask you very quietly whether the children on their ship will still get fed.

When they join the fight:
- {Enemy} pushes out of the crowd behind you, with the same hollow look, no longer caring who sees.

When they're stunned:
- {Enemy} stares at you and shakes, and can't make themselves come any closer.

When persuading works:
- You ask about the children on their ship. They start to answer, and then they're crying, and the knife's on the deck between you.
- You say you're not going to report this. They look at the knife as if someone else had put it there, and they let it fall.

When paying works:
- You hold out the credits, and they take them in both hands. They say they're sorry again, and they keep saying it as they back into the crowd.

When a threat works:
- You don't have to say much. They were never going to do it, and now they know you know. They drop the knife, and they run.
