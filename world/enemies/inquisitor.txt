A hunter of Resonants, with a blade that drinks energy.

When the fight starts:
- An Inquisitor in Ministry grey draws a pale blade, and addresses you politely by your full name.

When they hit:
- The blade opens a line across you, and you feel your strength drain into it.
- The Inquisitor steps through your guard and the pale blade drinks.

When they miss:
- The pale blade stops a finger's width from your throat.
- You twist away, and the blade hums hungrily past you.

When they hit hard:
- The blade sinks in, and everything you have goes cold and far away.

When they're beaten:
- The Inquisitor sinks to one knee and says, very quietly, that this changes nothing.
