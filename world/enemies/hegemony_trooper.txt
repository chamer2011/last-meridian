In a line:
Polished armor, perfect discipline, no doubts.

When the fight starts:
- A Hegemony trooper in polished armor advances at a measured pace and orders you to lie down.

When they hit:
- A precise burst hits you center mass, with no wasted rounds.
- The trooper steps forward and fires again, in exactly the same rhythm.

When they miss:
- Rounds pass close enough to feel, in a tight and tidy pattern.
- The trooper corrects a fraction, unhurried, and fires again.

When they hit hard:
- A single round finds the gap, as though the trooper had been told where it was.

When they're beaten:
- The trooper falls without a sound, and the polished armor rings on the deck.

When a lie works:
- You rattle off a clearance code, a section and a superior's name, with just enough impatience. The trooper checks their slate, finds nothing, and doesn't want to be the one who asks.
