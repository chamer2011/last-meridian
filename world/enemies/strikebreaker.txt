In a line:
Company muscle at Cinder Reach, paid by the shift, with the union patch cut off their helmet.

When the fight starts:
- A wide figure in a company vest hefts a length of drill rod, and tells you the refinery would like you to go home.
- Someone in a miner's helmet, with the union patch cut off it, steps into your way. They won't meet your eyes, but they don't move.

When they hit:
- The drill rod comes down across your shoulder, with ten years of mine work behind it.
- They drive you into the rock wall with a shoulder, hold you there, and hit you again.
- The helmet lamp blinds you for a moment, and the rod arrives out of the glare.

When they miss:
- The rod rings off the pipe beside your ear, and the whole tunnel hums with it.
- You duck, and the rod buries itself in a slag barrel. They swear at you while they pull it out.
- They swing low, you hop over it, and they stumble on the loose rock.

When they hit hard:
- The rod catches you behind the knee, then again on your way down, and they look almost sorry.

When they're beaten:
- They sit down hard, pull off the helmet, and tell you they've got a family to feed, the same as anyone.
- The rod clatters away from you down the tunnel, and they don't go after it.

When they join the fight:
- {Enemy} shoulders in from a side tunnel with a drill rod and a bonus to earn, and looks you over.

When they're stunned:
- {Enemy} shakes their head, the helmet lamp wobbling, and can't find you in the beam.
