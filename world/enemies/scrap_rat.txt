In a line:
A rat the size of a dog, grown fat on the insulation of dead ships.

When the fight starts:
- Something the size of a dog squeezes out of a torn duct, trailing chewed cable, and looks at your boots with interest.
- You hear small claws in the wall beside you. Then a panel falls off, and they aren't small.

When they hit:
- It runs straight up your leg, and teeth made for shielded cable close on your arm.
- Yellow teeth clamp on your calf, and it hangs on while you try to shake it off.
- It drops on you from a conduit overhead, all claws and stink.

When they miss:
- It lunges for your ankle, and you kick it into the bulkhead.
- Its teeth snap shut on your sleeve and come away with nothing but a mouthful of cloth.
- It darts between your feet and out the other side, squealing at you.

When they hit hard:
- It gets in under your collar and goes for your throat, and for a moment all you can hear is squealing.

When they're beaten:
- It flips onto its back at your feet, and its legs keep running for a while.
- You stop it with your boot. Somewhere in the walls, a lot of small feet go quiet.

When they join the fight:
- {Enemy} squeezes out of a torn duct behind you and comes for your ankles.

When they're stunned:
- {Enemy} shakes its head, staggers in a circle, and forgets about you.

When calming works:
- You kick a length of old insulation toward it and walk the other way. The rat falls on it, and it forgets you ever existed.
