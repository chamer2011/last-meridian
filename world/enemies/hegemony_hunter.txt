In a line:
A licensed hunter with a Hegemony warrant, a net gun and a long memory.

When the fight starts:
- A patient person in a long coat holds up a warrant with your face on it, and asks whether you'd rather come in awake.
- You know the sound of a net gun being primed. You've just never heard one pointed at you.

When they hit:
- The net wraps your arm and tightens, and the current in it takes the strength out of you.
- A weighted line snaps around your legs, you hit the deck, and the hunter reads you the warrant number.
- The hunter's other barrel isn't a net, as you now discover.

When they miss:
- The net sails past you and wraps around a pipe, which it tightens on until the pipe bends.
- You roll clear. The hunter reels the net back in without hurrying, and checks it for tears.
- The shot goes wide of you, and the hunter makes a small note of how you dodge.

When they hit hard:
- The net takes you square, and everything you try only pulls it tighter. "Alive pays better," the hunter says, "but not by much."

When they're beaten:
- The hunter sits down against the wall and hands you the warrant. "Someone else will pick this up," they tell you.
- The hunter falls, and the warrant slides out of their coat, with your picture and a much higher number than you'd expected.

When they join the fight:
- {Enemy} comes in behind you with a net gun primed, and calls out your full name to see whether you'll turn.

When they're stunned:
- {Enemy} fumbles with a tangled reel of line, and looks at you with professional embarrassment.
