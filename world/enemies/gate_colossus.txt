The Pale Gate's last guardian, as old as the ring itself and just as hard to argue with.

When the fight starts:
- The wall you've been walking beside for an hour stands up. It keeps standing up for longer than you'd like, and then it looks down at you.
- Far above you, lights older than any language come on in a line. A shape the size of a docking clamp steps away from the ring, and the floor bows under it.

When they hit:
- A hand the size of a shuttle door sweeps you off your feet and into the far wall.
- It stamps, the whole deck jumps, and you come down hard and badly.
- A bar of pale light crosses the hall, slow enough to see, and you still can't get out of its way.

When they miss:
- The fist comes down where you were, and the impact lifts you off the floor.
- You run between its legs, and it turns to follow you, slow as a moon rising.
- It sweeps one arm through the space above you, and the wind of it nearly takes you off your feet.

When they hit hard:
- It picks you up, studies you for a moment without any malice at all, and puts you down much too hard.

When they're beaten:
- The lights go out along its body, one by one, and it settles to its knees with a sound you feel more than hear.
- It stops. Far above you, something that has been awake for a very long time finally closes its eyes.

When they join the fight:
- The far wall of the chamber stands up and becomes {enemy}, and you have time to wish you'd run.

When they're stunned:
- {Enemy} stands locked in place with lights crawling over it, as if it's asking something far away about you.
