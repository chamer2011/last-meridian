HOW TO WRITE A FACTION
======================

Every faction is one file in this folder, named by its id: fleet.txt is the Refugee Fleet, and people, places,
enemies and stories name factions by that id. The Story board in the game (The story, then Story board, then
Factions) shows and changes the same files, and npm run check-story reads them all and says what's wrong, with the
file and the line. The four the game always knew, fleet, hegemony, drift and ilun, are the ones every captain
starts out knowing; the rest come up as the story goes on.

    # The Salvage Guild
    order: 5                            (where it comes in lists; leave it out to come after the rest, by name)
    also called: the Salvage Guild, the Guild
    starting standing: 0                (how a new captain stands with them, from -100 to 100)

    In a line:
    Wreck-divers who live by iron contracts and settle everything in their own courts.

    Beliefs:
    authority -4, loyalty 5, belief -3  (how far they lean on each axis, -10 to 10: authority, loyalty, violence,
                                         belief, flesh; the game compares them with the player's own)

A part that has nothing in it can be left out.
