HOW TO WRITE A SPECIES
======================

Every kind of people a character can be is one file in this folder, named by its id: ilari.txt is the Ilari, and
the people in world/characters name their species by that id. The character creator offers them in the order their
files give. The Story board in the game (The story, then Story board, then Species) shows and changes the same
files, and npm run check-story reads them all and says what's wrong, with the file and the line.

    # Ilari
    order: 4                            (where it comes in the character creator's list)
    extra attribute points: 2           (what a new character of this species gets to spend; leave it out for none)
    extra trait points: 1
    ageless terms: 8                    (how many terms of a career before age starts to tell; leave it out for the usual)

    In a line:
    Empaths whose skin carries slow patterns of light that show what they feel.

    For the game master:
    Ilari: skin patterns of soft light shift with emotion, so feelings are hard to hide.

    Attributes:
    resonance 2, presence 1, willpower -1          (added to a new character's attributes)

    Attribute caps:
    resonance 1                                     (an attribute that can't go above this; leave it out for none)

    Skills:
    attunement 1                                    (skill ranks a new character starts with)

    Effects:
    max hp: 5                           (more or fewer hit points)
    fatigue rate: 0.25                  (fractions of the usual: 0.5 is half)
    heal rate: 0.5
    stress rate: 0.85
    in a setting: heavy_gravity 3, vacuum 1         (easier or harder in a setting: an id from the contexts list)
    standing with: fleet 5, hegemony -15            (how factions start out feeling about them, by faction id)
    skills: deception -2, persuasion 1              (a bonus or penalty to a skill)

In a line is what the player reads when choosing. For the game master is how the AI plays them. A part that has
nothing in it can be left out.
