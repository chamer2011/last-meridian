# Ilari
order: 4

In a line:
Empaths whose skin carries slow patterns of light that show what they feel. They make wonderful friends and terrible liars.

For the game master:
Ilari: skin patterns of soft light shift with emotion, so feelings are hard to hide. Naturally sensitive to Resonance, which makes them a target for Hegemony Inquisitors.

Attributes:
resonance 2, presence 1, willpower -1

Skills:
attunement 1

Effects:
standing with: hegemony -15, ilun 10
skills: deception -2, persuasion 1
