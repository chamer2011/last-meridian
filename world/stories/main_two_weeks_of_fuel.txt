# Two Weeks of Fuel
kind: main
places: kessara, haven, cinder
people: quill, tannis, marrow, talia, ulla, corvane
factions: drift, fleet, hegemony

The lead:
Chief Marrow has done the sums three times, swearing louder each time: the fleet has two weeks of fuel. Mother Quill has sent over a basket of dumplings and an invitation to discuss it, dear.

For anyone who isn't the captain:
The fleet's down to two weeks of fuel, and everyone on the docks knows it. Mother Quill has been sending over baskets of dumplings, and a fleet that desperate will sign anything.

What's really going on:
Quill owns the only depot within reach big enough to fill forty-one ships, and she knows it. She'll sell on credit: fuel now, forty-one blank markers, one signed by the master of each ship and each good for a favor to be named later (see out_cinder_three_prices), and first look at any First Builder data the captain ever finds. She says the Beacon is the biggest score in history, an unclaimed First Builder warehouse, and she'd like a partner. That's partly true. What she really wants is pinned to her shawl: a chip of carved pale stone, part of the payment she took six years before the Fall for the Navy's rotation tables, from a buyer she never identified. She doesn't know who she sold them to. She has been buying everything First Builder ever since to find out, and hoping the answer isn't what she fears. Rook Tannis wants the Syndicate for herself and would happily let a desperate captain raid the depot and take the blame. Other sources exist, and all of them cost something: Cinder Reach has the fleet's best fuel and a strike (Ulla Greaves won't scab for anyone, and she doesn't know that Quill's money, through three shell charities, is what keeps her strike kitchen open), and Corvane will give it free, with a Hegemony transponder fitted to every tank. Arc clues: quill_pin.

How it might go:
- Tea in Quill's Parlor, on embroidered cushions, with a very quiet person in the corner holding her rifle for her. She asks after the captain's mother by name, and insists they eat something. The contract is one page long, which should worry anyone.
- Marrow's numbers get worse: a coolant leak on the Sundial, and a ship that has been stealing fuel to sell. He wants a real answer by tomorrow, and he'd like it in words he can swear at.
- Tannis buys the captain a drink on Raiders' Row and mentions, laughing, which night the depot guards change, and who she'd hate to see blamed for it.
- The competing offers arrive: Corvane's free fuel, or Ulla Greaves's terms if the captain will take the strikers' side against Director Vale. The council has opinions.
- Signing, raiding or walking away, with the pin catching the light every time she leans in to pat their hand.

Ways through:
- Talker: haggle with Quill (commerce, persuasion), put a limit on the markers in writing, or play her against Corvane. She respects a good bargain and she'll remember it.
- Scoundrel: forge a manifest, run the depot's books against her (deception, streetwise), or go through Talia Sen's noodle stall to learn what Quill's fences are really buying. Eat first.
- Fighter: take the depot with Tannis's help (tactics, gunnery, melee). It works. Then the Syndicate is at war with the fleet, and Tannis has a captain to blame.
- Technical: Marrow and the captain stretch what they've got (engineering, science, navigation): a cold-burn schedule that adds four days, at a cost in comfort and risk, and lets them bargain from less desperate ground.
- Political: go to Cinder Reach instead and settle a strike between Ulla Greaves and Director Vale (leadership, persuasion). It's the slowest and cleanest fuel there is.

If it comes to a fight:
- drift_raider and syndicate_collector if the depot raid goes wrong
- strikebreaker if the captain gets between the company and the picket line at Cinder Reach

Where it can end up:
sign_with_quill: If they sign with Quill, the fleet has fuel, parcels of food keep arriving, and people start calling her Grandmother. She'll call in the markers one at a time, at the worst possible moments, and much later she'll want silence about her ledger.
raid_depot: If they raid the depot, the fleet is fueled and at war with the Syndicate. Tannis owes them or has framed them. Kessara is closed unless they fix it, and later her ledger is harder to reach by talk and easier by force.
take_corvanes_fuel: If they take Corvane's fuel, it's free, and every ship in the fleet carries a Hegemony transponder until someone finds them. Halm's position is stronger, and Inspector Moor starts turning up wherever the fleet goes.
settle_cinder_reach: If they settle Cinder Reach, the supply is steady and honest, and either Rennick Vale or Ulla Greaves, whichever lost, will never forgive it. Fuel arrives late, and a ship or two is lost waiting for it.
