# The Credentials Act
kind: crew
places: meridian, fleet
people: pell, voss, halm
factions: fleet
level: 2

The lead:
The council's new Credentials Act makes every officer and doctor in the fleet prove their training by month's end, after a fake surgeon killed a man on the Harrow's Bounty. Ensign Asante just asked if she could talk to you, and forgot to add Captain at all.

For anyone who isn't the captain:
The council's new Credentials Act makes every officer and doctor in the fleet prove their training by month's end, after a fake surgeon killed a man on the Harrow's Bounty. Ensign Asante has gone very quiet about it.

What's really going on:
Pell never finished flight school. She forged her certification during the evacuation of Aurelis because no one else could fly the ship, and she got two thousand people out. The Act isn't aimed at her. It's a good law, sponsored by Councillor Ruth Amadi, whose husband was the man who died. It also threatens one credential in five, since the records burned in the Fall, and Halm likes having a lever on every officer. The twist: Voss has known since the first week. She read Pell's transcript, saw what was missing and countersigned it anyway, a court-martial offense from the last person anyone would suspect. "We had a pilot or we had a rule." The fleet's last licensed flight examiner is Ioan Beck, who now runs a ferry on the Second Thursday. He flunked Pell in her first term for reckless flying, and he has never forgotten her.

How it might go:
- Pell's confession, at speed, with "sir" arriving a beat late. She offers to turn herself in. She asks only that someone get her brother moved first.
- Councillor Amadi isn't a villain. She'd like the captain to come and see where her husband's bunk was. The first hearings start next week.
- The captain learns Voss countersigned. She doesn't deny it, and asks if that's all.
- Beck offers a practical exam, in a shuttle, under his eye. Partway through, a real call comes in: a lifeboat tumbling in the fleet's debris trail with a dead drive. The exam is now that.
- The hearing, if it comes to that. The gallery fills with people off Aurelis who never knew her name until today.

Ways through:
- The exam: piloting and navigation in the co-seat while Pell threads the debris, with Beck marking a clipboard throughout. Gunnery to clear the way when a slab won't move.
- The law: lore to find the wartime field commission clause that lets a captain certify an officer. It's real, but it requires a sworn statement of what the captain knew, which Halm will read aloud someday.
- The cover-up: hacking to patch her record a second time, and deception to Councillor Amadi's face. It works, and the captain has become the thing the Act exists to stop.
- The truth: leadership and persuasion at the hearing. Pell stands up and says it. The Act could survive that, if someone writes in a way for people to earn what they can't prove.

Checks that fit:
- piloting:hard to bring the lifeboat in through the debris
- navigation:moderate to plot a way into the trail
- gunnery:hard to shatter the slab blocking the way without hitting the lifeboat
- lore:moderate to find the field commission clause
- hacking:moderate to patch her record without leaving a seam
- persuasion:hard to get Councillor Amadi to write in a way to earn a credential

Where it can end up:
passes_becks_exam: She passes Beck's exam: Beck signs, hands her his old gloves, and tells her she's still reckless and still takes the third-best lane. She's a real pilot on paper now, and something in her shoulders comes down an inch.
