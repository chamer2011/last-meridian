The lead:
Warden Ashael of Lumen hasn't shown a single point of light on his skin since the Fall, and in a city where nobody can hide a feeling, no one dares ask him why. His spy, Mirei, has asked you to do it, because no one here can stand to.

For anyone who isn't the captain:
Warden Ashael of Lumen hasn't shown a single point of light on his skin since the Fall, and in a city where nobody can hide a feeling, no one dares ask him why. His spy, Mirei, is looking for someone who will, because no one here can stand to.

What's really going on:
In the last hour of the Fall the Lumen Circle reached out to hold the dying, with Ashael at its center, the strongest empath alive. He felt something under the Choir's song: huge, old and not the Choir, turning over in its sleep. The fear was too large to show. Feeling spreads skin to skin at Lumen, and the city is built on a First Builder conduit that amplifies whatever passes through it. If that had lit up in him, the panic would have gone through the city and killed people. So he went to Mirei, the only one who knew how, and had her teach him to go dark. He has held it down for seven months, and it's killing him by inches. He says aloud what his skin used to show: 'I say this with gladness: you are welcome here.' He's the biggest liar at Lumen, and it's an act of love. Abbess Ione knows. He's looking for someone strong enough to be told, and for someone to tell him it can be stopped. People have started to whisper he's a copy. Mirei alone knows why he asked, and she won't say. She wants this done because she's watching him die, and because she has spent years trying to find out what the Quiet did to her sister, Yara Solune, and she has noticed something: Yara's glow never varies. The Circle's theory of the Beacon is that it's grief, and they're right, as far as that goes. Arc clues: circle_hears_grief, ashael_felt_it, silent_unwanted.

How it might go:
- Lumen: a city of soft moving light where every passer-by shows you how they feel about you. A whole street changes colour when news goes down it. A refugee's fear shows up on strangers' skin three streets away, people keep apologizing for it, and the Shade Houses are full.
- Mirei: a blank, perfectly still Ilari in a city of lamps. Other Ilari step around her, very politely. 'You're frightened. It's in your shoulders. Go on.' She lays out the situation in an even voice, and asks which answer would be most useful to you.
- The Warden, at the top of the Open Steps, unlit, or at home in a dark room: courteous, with his jaw tight. He'll talk about anything else, and he asks after your family and means it. Ask him plainly, and he stops. Aduro, if they've come, takes his wrist and says nothing for a long time.
- The answer, or the touch: the captain gets a second of it, through a lumen_lens or through Attunement. Something the size of the sky turning over. Then Ashael's hand comes down, and the room is just a room.
- What should he do with it? Tell the city? Keep carrying it until it kills him? The Circle could share the load, each carrying a small part, in shifts. It would hurt them all, and it would work. Nobody has tried anything like that for a very long time. What do you tell a man who's holding that up alone?

Ways through:
- Talker: ask him (persuasion, composure). There's no trick. Nobody has done it. Being from outside helps, and so does not looking away.
- Doctor: bring Aduro or be one (medicine). He hasn't eaten in days, and it starts there. His body is a way in.
- Resonant: touch what he's holding down (attunement). You'll understand everything, and you'll be a wreck for a while. Manifestation won't help, because there's nothing to push, though at Lumen it will spread further than you meant.
- Spy: work with Mirei among the Quiet (stealth, deception, awareness): what she has found, and what she isn't telling you. A captain who can't sense anything can still read a spy's files. The lumen_lens is hers to lend.
- Leader: go over his head. Talk to the Circle on the Open Steps and get them to ask him together (leadership, persuasion). In a city where nothing can be hidden, everyone has been politely not asking for seven months.

Where it can end up:

It's the first time anyone has relieved anyone of anything in a very long while, and nobody notices the pattern yet. Lumen becomes the captain's ally.
keeps_secret: If he keeps it secret, he goes on, thinner. The captain now knows there's something under the song, and has to decide what to do with that.
city_told: If the city is told, half of Lumen wants to run and the other half wants to go and see. The panic is as bad as he feared, for a day, and fights between people who can't hide what they feel are very hard to watch.
mirei: Mirei: if the captain earns it, she shows one flicker of light on her wrist, once, and hides it. She's the best friend the fleet will ever have in the spy trade, and she has a question about her sister that someone will eventually have to answer.
