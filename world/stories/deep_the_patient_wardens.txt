# The Patient Wardens

The lead:
The Pale Gate has been dead for longer than anyone has been counting. Navigator Tamsin Ao-Rel swears the ring has moved four degrees since they last charted it, and that something on it turned its head to watch them go.

For anyone who isn't the captain:

What's really going on:
The Pale Gate is a ring the size of a moon, one seamless pale metal like old bone, and everyone assumes it's a gate to somewhere. It's a relay on the old road, a bell and a customs house. Struck properly, it's heard everywhere the song is. It was the checkpoint for relief crews on their way to the Harbor, the only sanctioned way through, and the gap in the Navy's rotation that the Tarn was sent to watch lies right beside it. Gate Wardens, slow First Builder automata, pace the Warden's Walk on rounds that take years. They aren't guarding the Gate against intruders. They're checking the crew in, and they've been holding their posts for a shift change that never came. They have one question: 'Whose relief are you?' Anyone carrying a gate_key, or who speaks properly in the words of the watch, is treated as staff. Saying you're the relief and meaning it also works, oddly. Force brings the Gate Colossus at the inner door, the last one who hasn't been told he can stand down. Inside are customs halls for peoples who no longer exist, benches for bodies nobody has ever seen, and the wrecks of everyone who ever tried to force it. Rimcamp, forty chancers and scholars, is welded to the one spar that will hold a weld, and it has one rule: sign the log going in, and whoever's on duty will come and look for you, once. Arc clues: blade_origin and overdue_crew are nearby, and the bearing passes through the Throat.

How it might go:
- The approach. You can't get a sense of its size until you realize the thin bright line along its inner face is a mountain range. Clocks disagree. Rimcamp has one still and one logbook, and everyone there has learned to ask the date politely.
- The Warden's Walk: corridors as wide as streets, thin cold air that smells of rain and dust that hasn't moved in forever. A customs hall, a bench, a queue rail for something with six feet.
- The first Warden. What you took for a pillar unfolds, one joint at a time. It has been looking at you for some time. It stops, and asks its question in a voice like a stone door: Whose relief are you?
- The wrecks: Hegemony scouts, Concord survey ships, Syndicate looters. Rusk can read them: who tried what, and how far they got. The last one got quite a long way. It looks as if they were allowed to, until they weren't.
- The bell. At the ring's keystone is a striker that's still free to move. You could ring it, and everyone in the song would hear. It can carry a killing note, a summons or an announcement of relief. The Throat, at the center, isn't always showing the same stars. And the wall you've been walking beside for an hour may stand up.

Ways through:
- Scholar: answer in the old words (lore), from the cut pages or the Tessen children's phrase, and walk through the front door.
- Talker: just tell it the truth (persuasion, composure): who you are, why you came and that you don't know what you're doing. Wardens don't judge an answer by its grammar, and a few of them have been waiting a long time to hear anything at all.
- Hacker: the Wardens are running a process so old it has fossils in it (hacking, science). Spoof a clearance with the Tuner's protocols or Seven's fragments. It'll hold until someone checks.
- Fighter: fight the Wardens and then the Colossus (firearms, melee, tactics, gunnery from the Meridian). It can be done. It's the biggest fight in the game, and the ring will remember it.
- Resonant: sing the pass (attunement, manifestation). The ring sings back, and your teeth hurt for a week.

Where it can end up:
pass_lawfully: If they pass lawfully, the Gate stands open for the captain's fleet and only for them. At the end, it's a fast and safe road.
force: If they force it, the way is open for anyone, including the Regent's squadron and anything else that wants to come through. The Wardens don't ask any more, and that should bother people.
bell_rung: If the bell is rung, everyone in the song hears it. Every Hunter stops for one second. Every Resonant in known space wakes at the same moment. The Cantor wants to know what the captain meant by it.
colossus_told_can_stand_down: If the Colossus is told he can stand down, by someone who means it, he does, and that's the first relief anyone has given in 1,006 years. Word of it runs down the Walk, one Warden at a time.
tamsin_charts: If Tamsin charts it, their maps prove the Gate isn't a way out. They might finally be believed, and that might be worse.
