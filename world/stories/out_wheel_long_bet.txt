# The Long Bet
kind: dilemma
places: wheel
people: oriel, kit
factions: drift, fleet
level: 2

The lead:
Madame Oriel Ao-Dess doesn't stand when you enter, since then she'd only have to look further down. She offers the fleet a full load of fuel, free, on one wager: that before this is over, you will knowingly leave one of your own ships behind to save the rest.

What's really going on:
Oriel doesn't cheat. She reads people, and she only bets on what people are. She has watched every evacuation, retreat and last stand for fifty years, and every commander eventually cuts a ship loose. The terms: the fleet gets real fuel now, and the captain carries a wheel_marker. If the captain ever knowingly abandons a fleet ship with people aboard, the marker comes due: one order, any order, given by Oriel at a time of her choosing, and the captain has to carry it out. If they reach the end of the road without doing it, she pays again, tenfold, in anything she owns. The GM should keep this bet alive across the whole game, and note it in the journal. What she doesn't say: everyone believes she's never lost a bet, and she has, once, at twenty-two, to Ilkin Ao-Vash, that an Ao-Vash convoy would never cut a family ship loose in the dark. It did, with the Ao-Dess aboard, and she's the only one who lived. She's been looking ever since for a commander who wouldn't have done it. She has also staked the whole station, at long odds, on the fleet reaching the Beacon, with Mother Quill on the other side, so this wager is really a way to learn which kind of captain she has backed. She would ruin the captain tomorrow if it improved the odds. Meanwhile, a bet this big leaks. Kit Marlowe-Dace is running a side book on which ship goes first, and somebody with money on the old freighter Widow's Lantern has decided to improve the odds.

How it might go:
- The offer, in Oriel's private room. She is unhurried and scrupulous, and she answers every question about the terms. "What counts as left behind?" "You'll know, Captain. So will I."
- Word gets out. In the fleet, the captains of slow, old ships start doing arithmetic, and a delegation arrives asking whether their lives have been staked at a casino.
- Kit shows up, full of apologies, to offer the captain a very good price against themselves, and lets slip the odds on the Widow's Lantern have moved for no good reason.
- The Widow's Lantern loses a coolant line in a very tidy way. Someone arranged it. Her reactor will need the fleet to stop and wait, right when the fleet shouldn't.
- The captain's first real test of the bet, with Oriel's marker warm in their pocket. There's a card from her afterward, either way.

Ways through:
- Take the bet and keep it: composure and leadership when the delegation arrives, then engineering and void_ops to save the Widow's Lantern the hard way.
- Beat the book: commerce, streetwise and deception with Kit to find out who's bet on sabotage, and ruin them by moving the odds.
- Refuse, or renegotiate: commerce or persuasion to change the terms, and awareness to read Oriel well enough to guess what she really wants.

Checks that fit:
- composure:moderate to read Oriel's terms without showing how much the fleet needs that fuel
- commerce:hard to get a clause that protects ships lost in battle
- streetwise:moderate to find out who put money on the Widow's Lantern
- engineering:hard to save the freighter's reactor in time
- leadership:moderate to calm the small ships' captains
- awareness:very_hard to catch the one moment when her fingers go to the old chip at her throat, and wonder what she lost

If it comes to a fight:
- dock_thug hired by the saboteur if the captain gets close, in the Wheel's service levels. Calling in Kit's debts sends them home without a blow.

Where it can end up:
captain_takes_bet: If the captain takes the bet, the fleet gets several jumps' worth of fuel at once, and every ship left behind later, by anyone's hand, comes with a card from Madame Oriel.
bet_lost: If the bet is lost, she calls the marker at the worst possible moment, and her order should be something the captain can obey and hate, never a dead end.
captain_makes_to_end_without_abandoning: If the captain makes it to the end without abandoning a ship, she pays in full and, for the first time anyone has seen, stands up.
captain_refuses_wager: If the captain refuses the wager, Oriel respects it and the fleet goes on looking for fuel. She keeps the offer open, and she has an excellent memory for moments of need.

If nobody steps in:
after 45 days: Madame Oriel has opened a book at the Gilded Wheel on which of the fleet's ships the captain would leave behind first, with the odds chalked up for anyone to read.
  lead: Madame Oriel's opened a book on it: which of your ships you'd leave behind first, with the odds chalked up for anyone to read. The offer still stands, a full load of fuel, free, on one wager. She still doesn't stand when you come in.
  fleet: morale -1

Rewards:
Up to two refined_fuel at once on accepting, plus a wheel_marker. Kit's friendship, for what that's worth. Saving the Widow's Lantern is worth fleet morale and the deep loyalty of the fleet's smallest ships.
