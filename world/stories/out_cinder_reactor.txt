For anyone who isn't the captain:
Director Rennick Vale, whom everyone on Cinder Reach would gladly throw in the slag, is going round with a bad map and a worse number. The reactor runs on what the miners dig, he says, and the stockpile has twelve days in it. After that the moon has three. He has said so before. Nobody believes a word he says.

What's really going on:
He's right. The Tap has been bleeding off the moon's magma pressure for sixty years. It runs on what the miners dig, so the strike is eating through the last of the stockpile. When the reactor stops, the pressure has nowhere to go, and three days later it comes up through Shift Row. His records are so full of old lies that nobody can prove it, and Ulla's engineers call it a company trick. The personal knot is in Ulla's jacket. Rennick's full report has been in there for a month, unread, because Ulla Greaves can't read. Nobody ever taught her. She's hidden it for forty years, her son Nils reads her everything, and Nils is out on the picket. Dunn Marrow could read Rennick's figures in a minute, and Rennick would give anything for that conversation. Nothing moves until she hears it from someone she trusts, and until Rennick says the thing she's waited to hear about Shaft Nine. She carried him out of a blowout in Shaft Two twenty-six years ago. He did shut that door. Up on Company Hill a shuttle sits fueled that seats three hundred, and he's written the list. His own name isn't on it, and the first two names are Ulla Greaves and his daughter. The last twist: even with the strike over, the reactor is failing. It needs a twelve-week controlled shutdown with four hundred trained people, or it fails on its own within a few years. There's no saving Cinder Reach as it is, only choosing how it ends.

How it might go:
- Rennick's number. Nobody believes it, and Ulla laughs, then stops. Dunn Marrow reads the plans over the comm and goes quiet, which is the frightening part.
- Proving it: a descent through the Tubes to the Tap with instruments, past hot-rock vents and things that eat heat, while the crowd up top holds a party because the Stack has finally gone silent.
- The report. A careful captain notices Ulla handing papers to her son, counting on her fingers, or holding the thing the wrong way up in a bad moment. How they handle that is the heart of this thread. She'd rather die than have it known.
- The first tremor on Shift Row. Cracks in the barracks floors. Someone finds the shuttle and the list, and the crowd wants to know whose names are on it. Rennick has to stand in front of the people who've sworn to kill him and say "I shut that door."
- The restart of the digging, with the two of them in one control room, and Rennick's other number, the twelve weeks. Now the captain knows what the fleet's best fuel supply really is, and has to decide who else should.

Ways through:
- Prove it with instruments: science and survival down in the Tubes, then composure and persuasion to lay the readings in front of a hostile crowd.
- Fix it with hands: engineering and void_ops with Marrow on the line to stretch the stockpile, or to rig relief vents another way, using whoever's still willing to pick up a spanner.
- Bring them together: leadership and medicine for a crowd in panic, persuasion to get Ulla and Rennick into one room, and a very quiet kindness or a very cruel threat about what Ulla can and can't read.

Where it can end up:
digging_restarts_shutdown_plan_agreed: If the digging restarts and the shutdown plan is agreed, the colony lives and has a future somewhere else, on a slow clock. Rennick is either cleared or dead of the strain, and the fleet gets a long fuel deal.
tap_vented_another_way: If the Tap is vented another way, with slag crawlers drawing off the heat as the Lost Shift discovered, the colony gets a much longer future than Rennick ever calculated, and it's very surprised.
fails: If it fails, Shift Row must be evacuated in days, and the fleet takes on thousands of miners, along with their skills and their anger. The Stack goes dark, and fuel gets very expensive everywhere.
captain_uses_ullas_secret_against_her: If the captain uses Ulla's secret against her, it works, and it's the worst thing they've done. If they keep it, and quietly offer to teach her, she's theirs for life, and she'll never mention it.
