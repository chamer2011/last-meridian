For anyone who isn't the captain:

What's really going on:
The bridge has been reading Lira's skin for months: it shows what the sensors found before she can speak, and Pell, her bunkmate, has started steering by the color of her arm without asking why. Lira covered up because her skin is telling on her. Nineteen days ago she picked up one faint contact shadowing the fleet at extreme range. When it pings, its transponder reads CFS Tarn, a ship lost in the first hour of the Fall. Every nineteen days it drops off her board for a few days, then comes back. She took it to Voss, who looked at it for a long time, then ordered her in writing not to log it. Lira is a good officer and she's following the order, and she can't stop her skin from saying so. She grew up at Lumen, where the spy Mirei once offered to teach her to go dark. She said no, and the sleeves are the nearest she can get. There's a second thing she hasn't let herself notice: the bridge recordings show her skin reacts about four minutes before a contact appears on her board, and has done for every Choir contact since the Fall. She's an unconscious Resonant, which is why she's the best sensor officer in the fleet, and the Hegemony hunts people for less. Lately, on the darkest watches, a slow pale pattern has been moving across her back that has nothing to do with any ship.

How it might go:
- The sleeves. Rumor reaches the lower decks within the hour: people have been betting on her colors for months, and now there's nothing to bet on. A run starts on lifeboat seats. The Meridian's helm has become very slightly worse, and Pell can't say why.
- Talking to Lira. She's very bad at this. "Confirming: I have been given an order. Please disregard the collar." Violet climbs out of it as she speaks.
- Voss, asked directly, answers directly: she gave the order, she can't explain it yet, and she'll accept whatever the captain does about it.
- Going to look: a long run out in a shuttle with Pell. A trail of broken Choir scouts, and something far off, turning to keep its distance.
- The bridge tapes: four minutes. Someone notices. Now the captain has to decide what Lira is, and who gets to know.

Ways through:
- Talk: persuasion and composure. Give her a way to tell you without breaking the order. She's waiting for someone to ask the right question.
- Analyze: science and hacking on the bridge tapes and sensor logs, finding the four minutes and the contact, with awareness to catch a pattern everyone else has stopped seeing.
- Go and look: piloting, navigation and gunnery in a long-range shuttle run down the fleet's wake.
- Recognize it: attunement knows another Resonant on sight, which raises what to do about it: send her to Abbess Ione for training, hide her, or keep using her.

Where it can end up:
contact_logged: The contact is logged: the bridge knows, Tarrow knows, and Voss's order is now a matter of record. It raises hard questions about Voss, and forces the issue of crew_voss_night_scans if that's still open.
stays_off_log_lira_told: It stays off the log and Lira is told she can take off her sleeves: the captain is now part of the order. She's steadier. She also isn't stupid, and she knows what that cost.
her_gift_used: Her gift is used: her skin becomes an official early warning, with a recorder on her at all times. The fleet gets four minutes' warning of every attack. She gets no privacy at all, and one day Inspector Moor asks a polite question about it.
goes_training: She goes for training: with Abbess Ione or Brother Cael, if the captain can arrange it and spare her. The fleet loses its best eyes for a while. Ione is very glad to have her, and the captain may later wish they'd asked why. Lira comes back knowing what the pale pattern is, which she may wish she didn't.
