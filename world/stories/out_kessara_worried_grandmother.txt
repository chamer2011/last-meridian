# A Worried Grandmother
kind: faction
places: kessara
people: quill, tannis, talia
factions: drift, fleet
level: 3

The lead:
Rook Tannis slaps you on the back hard enough to rattle your teeth and offers cheap fuel for one easy escort job. An hour later Mother Quill pours you tea and says she's worried about dear Rook, and wouldn't it be a kindness if you took that job and told her how it went?

For anyone who isn't the captain:
Rook Tannis slaps people on the back hard enough to rattle their teeth and offers good money for one easy escort job. An hour later Mother Quill pours them tea, and says she's worried about dear Rook.

What's really going on:
Tannis is staging a coup. The escort job puts the Meridian's transponder at Quill's fuel depot at the exact moment her raiders seize it, so that the Syndicate will blame the fleet. Quill has known for months. She wants the coup to start, so that she can crush it in front of everyone and use the Meridian's presence as grounds to foreclose on the whole fleet. So the two most dangerous people in Kessara have, separately and cheerfully, chosen the same scapegoat. Neither knows the other has. Tannis has quietly bought up eleven debts on the Tally Wall owed by Quill's oldest lieutenants, so on the day she moves they'll stand aside. Talia Sen knows all of it, because their people eat at her stall, and she has her own stake: her son Davi crews on the Second Opinion, and she'd sell nearly anyone to get him off it before the day comes. The swashbuckling answer is that while both of them are busy framing the captain, the depot's guards, tankers and pumps will all be looking the wrong way for about ninety minutes.

How it might go:
- Two friendly offers in one afternoon. Each is generous. Each comes with a small detail that doesn't quite fit: a time that can't change, or a docking clamp that has to be the Meridian's own.
- Talia's stall. She lays out two soup spoons and a chopstick on the counter and shows the captain exactly who is standing where, and which one of them is the captain. Her price is one young deckhand, taken off the Second Opinion before it starts.
- Everyone in the warren starts being nice. Raiders buy the crew drinks, and Quill sends a shawl. The clock on the job is running.
- The day. The transponder is wherever the captain has decided to put it, Tannis's raiders move, Quill's trap springs, and the depot is suddenly and briefly nobody's.
- The aftermath, in Quill's parlor or on the deck of the Second Opinion, or both. Someone is going to say "dear" and mean something by it.

Ways through:
- Back a side and do it well: tactics and intimidation with Tannis, or deception and commerce with Quill, and take the reward from whoever wins.
- Turn them on each other: streetwise and deception to let each one learn just enough about the other, and keep the fleet out of the blast.
- Rob the depot: stealth, piloting and void_ops to fill the fleet's tankers during the ninety minutes, while a decoy broadcasts the Meridian's transponder somewhere very visible.

Checks that fit:
- streetwise:moderate to get a straight account from Talia's network
- deception:hard to keep smiling at Mother Quill while she tells you about her worries
- tactics:moderate to read the coup's timing from the raiders' dock movements
- hacking:hard to clone the transponder onto a decoy
- intimidation:hard to tell Tannis to her face that you won't take the fall
- firearms:hard to shoot out the depot's pump controls from a catwalk and be gone before the smoke clears

If it comes to a fight:
- drift_raider at the depot, if the captain is seen and stays to fight. The whole point of the robbery route is to be gone first.
- tannis herself, using her character file, only if the captain insists. She'd enjoy it, and she'd stop if they made her laugh.

Where it can end up:
tannis_wins: If Tannis wins, the fleet gets cheap fuel for one season and a pirate queen who thinks of the fleet as her first conquest. Mother Quill isn't dead unless the player's choices earned it. She's vanished, and she's still sending birthday cards.
quill_wins: If Quill wins, she rewards the captain handsomely in public, and the fleet's debt quietly grows by a clause. Tannis, in chains or on the run, remembers who talked.
wreck_each_other: If they wreck each other, the depot may burn. The Syndicate is weakened for months, which everybody enjoys until the fleet needs fuel, and Talia Sen finds herself with far more influence than she wanted.
captain_robs_depot_gets_away_with: If the captain robs the depot and gets away with it, the fleet has fuel for weeks. Both of them eventually work it out, both admire it, and neither forgets.
