The lead:
The freighter Patience of Ostra has stopped answering. Ensign Vey's skin went white when she scanned it and she still hasn't said why. All two hundred people aboard are reading as alive.

For anyone who isn't the captain:
The freighter Patience of Ostra has stopped answering. Ensign Vey's skin went white when she scanned it and she still hasn't said why. All two hundred people aboard are reading as alive.

What's really going on:
Everyone on the Patience has sat down, in corridors, at tables and in the engine room, all facing the same way. They're alive and breathing, and nobody's home. It happened in one night. This is what's at the far end of the Quiet's peace. The song is a lullaby made for something the size of the sky, and as it frays it spills. A little takes away fear. But the peace at the end isn't the song at all. It's what comes through the gaps in it: go far enough and a mind goes so still that what's on the other side can hear it, and then there's nobody there. A Choir Hunter that has tailed the fleet for weeks changes course to avoid the ship, the first time anyone has seen it avoid anything. A Mimic walks past a seated child as though no one's there. The Cantor, if asked, sounds afraid for the first time: 'There is nothing there to keep.' So the Quiet were never being called, and the Choir doesn't want them. Yara Solune's glow never varies, because the Choir began copying her and stopped, and nothing is left underneath it. It keeps time with Pavel Ostrov's hum. Oskar Lind comes aboard looking for his congregation, and he's the one who asks: can they be brought back? If they haven't been gone long, yes: by draining them with a Proctor's blade (Vael's weapon, of all things), or slowly, by someone who knew them calling their name. Tessaly Marsh got there first. She has seen this twice before on frontier moons, and she's already pricing them. Some of the seated have got up and walk now, as Silent Ones, and they don't like being moved. Arc clues: silent_unwanted, silent_bearing, blade_origin.

How it might go:
- The boarding. Someone's cooking has long gone cold on a stove. Two hundred people, all facing aft and to port, and none of them look unhappy. The team's voices drop to a whisper without anyone deciding to. Tessaly Marsh looks up from a patient: 'Don't tell me, I'll guess. You thought it was catching.'
- The bearing. Lira plots it. 'Confirming: it's the same from anywhere in the fleet.' It's the bearing of the lattice's source. On her board, the Hunter's track bends away.
- Someone on the boarding team sits down. Getting them up is the first real test of what can be done, and what it costs.
- Oskar arrives, shaken, with a list of names: 'They've been kept. Yes. They've been kept, haven't they?' Yara follows, glowing, and says it's all right. A lumen_lens or a Resonant shows that her glow hasn't changed in months. Mirei, her sister, watches from the airlock with no expression at all.
- What does the fleet do with two hundred people who need water and don't need anything else? Scuttle, quarantine or tow them? Water's rationed, and Aduro and Wen are on opposite sides for once. If it can happen to this ship, it can happen to all of them.

Ways through:
- Medical: work out what it is (medicine, science). They aren't sick, and that's the terrible part. Find out who can be woken and how. Tessaly knows more than she's saying, and she'll trade.
- Resonant: listen to the stillness on purpose, roped to someone who'll pull you out (attunement, composure). It feels like putting down everything you've ever carried. Manifestation can shove someone back out by force, at a price.
- Talker: get Oskar and what's left of the Quiet to face it (persuasion, lore). A preacher who turns is worth a thousand arrests. Or go to Mirei, who can show the captain the whole network and wants one thing in return: to know what happened to Yara.
- Practical: tow, quarantine and guard the ship (void_ops, engineering, tactics, leadership) and keep the fleet from panicking. Someone has to make the reactor safe, stepping around the seated.
- Force: capture Vael or take a drinking blade and try it (melee, medicine). Nobody expects the Inquisitor's weapon to be the cure, and nobody can say how much it will hurt.

Where it can end up:
oskar_turns: If Oskar turns, there's a slow exodus from the Quiet back into the fleet, and he's the captain's most unlikely ally. He'll be the first to volunteer, at the end, for anything that might reach his daughter. Yara doesn't turn. She sits beside the frightened until someone stops her.
patience_scuttled: If the Patience is scuttled, the fleet's water is saved. Everyone aboard is lost to everything: not dead, not kept, just gone. Aduro may never speak to the captain again, and the Quiet splits.
shes_kept: If she's kept, it's costly, and some are woken. Each one woken is a witness and says the same thing: 'I wasn't unhappy. Please understand. I wasn't anything.'
vael_asked: If Vael is asked, or has turned, she comes aboard with her gloves off and uses the blade for what it was made for, for the first time in a thousand years. A seated woman gasps and starts to cry. To find her, Vael has to take off her dampener band, and everyone in the room who can feel such things learns what she is. She apologizes.
news_gets_out_through_garrick: When the news gets out through Garrick Fenn, nobody's in any doubt that something besides the Choir is out there. Fear of the Quiet turns into a pogrom on three ships, unless the captain prevents it.
