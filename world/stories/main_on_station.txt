The lead:
Rusk, the diver with two dead partners, has sent word that he'll finally show you the map he won't finish a sentence about. He has one question first: does anybody on your ship remember what a Navy bell sounds like?

For anyone who isn't the captain:
Rusk, the diver with two dead partners, is telling anyone who buys the drink about a map he won't finish a sentence about. He has one question first: does anybody remember what a Navy bell sounds like?

What's really going on:
CFS Tarn is still on her picket. Silver has grown over her hull, and sixty-three silver figures stand her watches with the faces of her crew. She shadows the living fleet at the edge of sensor range, breaking any Choir scout that gets too close, and every nineteen days she goes back to her assigned station beside the broken Home Fleet, in the Long Silence, and rings the watch change on her brass bell. Rusk's chart of the battle's last hour ends at that spot, where there's no wreck, and where his partner Ansa's voice stopped four months ago, after reading out ships and bearings for nine hours past the end of her air. Ansa is on the watch bill now. Elsbeth Crane holds a dummy claim on the ship. The deck log tells the story: '0348: Cdr Voss relieved. Cdr off watch, turned in. 0359: Boarded. 0402: To boarders: the Commander is off watch and is not to be woken. 0403: Boarders acknowledge.' Lt. Nell Ibarra, last on her feet, said what an officer would say, and the Choir, whose deepest law is the watch bill, recognized it. It kept this crew together instead of scattering them through the song, and it learned to talk from them. They are the Cantor. The signal log shows Comms Tech Anja Sorel, three weeks after, running the lattice through the Navy's codebook, getting SAFE HARBOR at eleven percent confidence, then adding the fleet recall herself. Nobody ordered that. In the command chair sits a silver figure with Voss's own face, who doesn't look up (see crew_voss_relieved). They aren't monsters, and they aren't well, and they'll still take anyone who starts trouble, gently. Ibarra to the captain: 'Standing our watch, sir. Someone has to. Nobody's come to relieve us.' She doesn't know what the watch is for. Arc clues: tarn_log, overlay_good_faith.

How it might go:
- Rusk on an airlock sill with two helmets on his harness. 'You've got eleven minutes of me. Dell says you'll run. Ansa says you won't, but Ansa's soft.' He needs a ship that can hold station and someone on the line who won't sell him to the Factor. Then Elsbeth Crane, with a contract: 'For the avoidance of doubt, that hull is under claim.'
- The Long Silence, wreck after wreck of the Home Fleet, on the nineteenth day. Then a bell, coming through the hull of your own ship. Then running lights, deck by deck, the way you'd show a guest to his room.
- Aboard. Silver crew going about their duties. They turn and stand aside, or they don't, depending on how the captain comes aboard. The bosun's whistle still works. The coffee's still terrible, and someone's still complaining about it.
- The log and the signal board. Iven on the ship's line with his sister: she has been reading the same list for months, and then she says something she has never said. 'You look terrible, Iv. Go to bed. I've got the watch.' If Voss is there, Ibarra's face: 'Commander. You're not on the watch bill.' And Voss, at last, returns the salute.
- What to do with it. Take Anja off the air and the fleet loses the last hope it has. Scuttle her and kill them twice. Leave her, and the lie keeps going out. Elsbeth wants her salvage, Rusk wants Ansa, and all sixty-three mouths ask the Commander if she's slept well.

Ways through:
- By the book: come alongside and request permission to come aboard in proper Navy form (leadership, composure, lore). The crew responds to form. The captain can walk in the front door, if no one panics.
- Hacker: spoof a fleet recognition signal with remnant_codes, ride the ship's own systems, and read both logs from a maintenance bay without facing anyone (hacking, stealth).
- Fighter: board in armor, hold the corridors and take the log by force (firearms, melee, tactics). They don't hate you. They keep coming anyway, more and more gently, and nobody has ever fought anything that apologizes so sincerely.
- Outside the hull: void_ops and stealth along the ship's spine to the comms shack's external access, with Rusk on the line, past the place where Ansa went in.
- Resonant: hear all sixty-three at once (attunement). They know each other. One is crying, one is telling a joke, and they all know the captain's there. Manifestation can swing the brass bell and ring a false watch change, and everyone will stop to take their stations.

Where it can end up:
voss_cleared_with_wholl_read: Voss is cleared with anyone who'll read the log, and her whole life changes: she knows why she lived, who did it, and what it cost. Tarrow has to decide whether she believes it. Voss asks the captain not to make her speak about it.
anja_told_to_stop: If Anja is told to stop, or taken off the air, the fleet hears the Beacon go back to nothing but the lattice, and there's panic. If she's left, the captain goes on letting that message go out, knowingly.
tarn_scuttled: If the Tarn is scuttled, her crew aren't ended, since they're in the song, but the Cantor is hurt and changed, later parleys are colder, and the wake of the fleet fills up with scouts. Elsbeth sues.
shes_left_station: If she's left on station, the captain can come back to her. Ibarra will answer a hail, and at the end she can lead the fleet down the song's own lanes.
iven_may_try_to_stay: Iven may try to stay aboard. If Voss or the captain orders him not to, or Anja does, he comes home and sleeps for three days. If he stays, the fleet has a link to the Tarn, and the captain has lost a friend.
rusk_finds_ansa: Rusk finds Ansa, on the watch bill, with her helmet off. She tells him Dell says hello, which she has no way of knowing. Whatever he does next, he finishes a sentence about the map.
everyone_aboard_now_knows_choir: Everyone aboard now knows the Choir obeys a watch bill. A Hunter told in proper Navy form, by a very calm voice, that someone is off watch will hesitate, and it may even acknowledge. It works a few times before it stops working, and it's a terrifying thing to try.
