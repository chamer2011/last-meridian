# Arbiter of Last Resort
kind: politics
places: haven
people: mott, sable, corvane, tannis
factions: drift, hegemony, fleet

The lead:
Dockmaster Mott Jessup walks up with a two-hundred-year-old station charter and a queue of angry people behind him. "Says here the senior Concord officer present has the final word on every dispute at Haven," he says. "Nobody's checked who that is lately. Captain, it's you."

For anyone who isn't the captain:
Dockmaster Mott Jessup has dug up a two-hundred-year-old station charter. It says the senior Concord officer present has the final word on every dispute at Haven, and there's a queue of angry people behind him, waiting to find out who that is.

What's really going on:
The clause is real and still binding, because nobody thought to repeal it. Mott dug it up because he has a dispute he can't settle and stay alive: a sealed cargo pod claimed by both Legate Corvane and Captain Tannis. He's using the captain as a shield. The twist is the pod itself: its lot numbers show it's Concord medical stock looted from Aurelis, so the fleet has the best legal claim of anyone, and an honest ruling will look exactly like a corrupt one. The charter also lets the arbiter set docking tariffs during an emergency, which could feed the fleet for months. Sable has known about the clause for years and kept quiet, because the Neutral Ground only works while nobody is in charge. Mother Quill holds the mortgage on Haven's docks through three shell companies, so any tariff comes out of her pocket first, and she'll worry about that very soon. The charter binds the arbiter's own ships too, so the next case in the queue is a fleet family suing the captain.

How it might go:
- The queue. Three small cases that show what the job is: a Veshti and a Korrun disputing a fee, a widow's claim to her dead husband's berth, and a child accused of stealing air filters. People obey the rulings, looking astonished.
- The pod. Corvane says diplomatic goods, Tannis says stolen Syndicate goods. Both smile, both make private offers, and both hint at what happens to a captain who rules the wrong way.
- Sable slides the tariff clause across the bar, along with a warning that costs nothing and is worth a lot: the day everyone believes someone runs Haven, Haven stops being worth drinking in.
- A fleet family files against the captain: the Meridian took their ship's reactor parts under emergency order, and the charter says the arbiter must hear it.
- The final ruling, in public: keep the power, spend it once, or give it away.

Ways through:
- Be a real judge: lore to read the charter closely, awareness to find the lot numbers, persuasion and leadership to make a ruling stick against two dangerous people.
- Be a corrupt one: commerce, deception and streetwise to sell verdicts to the highest bidder and turn the clause into fuel money, for as long as it lasts.
- Give it away: help Mott and Sable draft a station council, using persuasion and lore, and step down with a lot of grateful people owing you.

Checks that fit:
- lore:moderate to find the tariff clause and its limits
- awareness:moderate to spot the Aurelis lot numbers on the pod
- persuasion:hard to make Corvane and Tannis accept a ruling against them
- commerce:moderate to set a tariff without starting a dock strike
- leadership:moderate to keep the queue from turning into a riot
- firearms:moderate to put one warning shot exactly where everyone can see it, in a station that hasn't heard one in years

If it comes to a fight:
- dock_thug hired by whoever lost the ruling, in a service corridor. A witness or a word to Sable's people ends it as well as a sidearm does.

Where it can end up:
pod_goes_to_fleet_with_proof: If the pod goes to the fleet with proof, Anselm's Mercy gets real medicine, Corvane congratulates the captain warmly and remembers, and Tannis laughs and sends a bill.
captain_keeps_tariff: If the captain keeps the tariff, the fleet earns fuel and credits on every visit, but the Neutral Ground stops being neutral: people bring guns to the bar, trade thins out, and Sable's prices double.
captain_hands_clause_to_station_council: If the captain hands the clause to a station council, Mott and Sable both owe a favor and the fleet gets free docking for good, but the captain has no more say at Haven.
however_fleet_familys_case_goes: However the fleet family's case goes, everyone hears about it. Ruling against themselves costs the captain a reactor part and earns real love in the fleet. Ruling for themselves is legal, and nobody forgets it.
