# Letters from Joss
kind: crew
places: meridian, anselm
people: naomi, pell, wen
factions: fleet
level: 3
after: crew_pell_certificate

The lead:
Naomi Cass, twelve years old and afraid of nothing on this ship, asks how you'd go about telling someone something if you'd already left it four months too long. She won't say who.

For anyone who isn't the captain:
Naomi Cass, twelve years old and afraid of nothing on this ship, has been asking how a person goes about telling someone something they've left four months too long. She won't say who.

What's really going on:
Four months ago a hull breach opened a section of the Anneke's Promise. A notice came through the mail for Ensign Asante: Joss Asante, fourteen, lost. Naomi carried it. That same morning she had watched Pell fly the fleet through a Choir sweep with shaking hands, she knows what it is to wait for a name on a manifest, and she couldn't hand it over. She hid it. When Pell's next letter to Joss came, Naomi answered it. She has now written sixteen letters in a careful boy's hand. Joss has opinions about engine noise and a running joke about soup. Pell reads them on the bridge, and they're part of why she can fly. She keeps asking the captain to get Joss onto a safer ship. The second twist, which only comes to those who dig: the notice may be wrong. Nine people were listed lost, but rescue crews pulled one unnamed boy out of that section and shipped him off in the chaos. On Anselm's Mercy, in Ward Six, bed forty holds a male of about fourteen with no tag, and he's on Dr. Adeyemi's list for the next support cut. If fleet_humming_ward is in play, he's humming.

How it might go:
- Naomi's question, sideways. She'll only say more if she's treated as a person and not a child. Then a box of letters, all her drafts, and the hidden notice.
- The captain watches Pell read the new one at the helm and laugh. The next jump is in two days, and it's a hard one.
- When to tell her: now, after the jump, never, and by whom. Naomi says she'll do it if she has to, and looks very small saying it.
- If anyone checks the record: there's no body and a bad count. The search leads to Anselm's Mercy and a row of unnamed beds.
- Bed forty. It might be him. Wen's list doesn't care. "Someone's brother. Every bed. Which one do I take out?"

Ways through:
- Tell her now: persuasion and composure, with the jump in two days and a pilot who may not hold together. The captain can't know until she's at the helm.
- Wait until after the jump: deception. She'll ask the captain straight out if something's wrong, and the captain is now part of the lie.
- Stand behind Naomi: leadership, a quiet room and a twelve-year-old who insists on doing it herself.
- Dig: awareness and hacking in Anselm's records, medicine to confirm who's in that bed, and attunement to try to reach him.

Checks that fit:
- hacking:moderate to pull the Anneke's real casualty record
- awareness:hard to notice the missing boy in the rescue log
- medicine:moderate to identify bed forty from old scans
- persuasion:hard to get Dr. Adeyemi to hold a bed
- deception:hard to lie to Pell's face when she asks you directly
- composure:moderate to read one of those letters to the end

Where it can end up:
shes_told_before_jump: She's told before the jump: she flies it anyway, shaking and white, then goes below and doesn't come back for two days. Then she does. She asks to keep the letters.
shes_told_after_jump: She's told after the jump: the fleet is safe. She asks how long the captain knew, to the hour, and it's a long time before she says "Captain" again, a beat late or otherwise.
naomi_tells_her: Naomi tells her: Pell doesn't shout. A week later she asks Naomi to teach her the crawlspaces, and nobody asks why. It becomes one of the best things on the ship.
bed_forty_him: Bed forty is him: he's kept on support at the expense of whoever was next, and that person has a name and a family. If he wakes, it's the best day anyone on the bridge has had since the Fall, and he may wake up humming.
nobody_ever_tells_her: Nobody ever tells her: the letters go on, and Naomi gets better at them. The captain should feel this get heavier in every scene.
