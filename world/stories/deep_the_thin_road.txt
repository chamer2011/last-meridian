# The Thin Road
kind: exploration
places: deepyards, ghostlight
people: tamsin, ilkin, pell, oriel
factions: families, fleet
level: 4

The lead:
Navigator Tamsin Ao-Rel will sell you the only chart there is to where the Beacon comes from, on one condition: you ask Yardmother Ilkin, in front of her family, what happened to Sel Arrah.

For anyone who isn't the captain:
Navigator Tamsin Ao-Rel will sell the only chart there is to where the Beacon comes from, on one condition: that the buyer asks Yardmother Ilkin, in front of her family, what happened to Sel Arrah.

What's really going on:
Nine generations ago, the Veshti lived on planets like everyone else. The Families say a blight drove them off (see out_deepyards_tamsins_chart). It wasn't a blight. Sel Arrah was their greatest world, and in one night its people sat down, about a hundred years after the Stilling: the first world to pay for it. The rest fled every planet they had, following the only thing they had, a First Builder route plate kept from the days when the Veshti held the watch, out to an ice ring formed around a First Builder frame, and they've told their children ever since that planets are filthy. It's why open skies make them queasy, and why every Veshti pilot knows a fixed light on all bands is a hazard. Tamsin copied the plate as a young navigator and flew as much of it as they dared. One route ends at the Pale Gate. Another runs out past the map, the same way the Beacon does, along buoys older than the Families: the Thin Road, the only way to the Beacon's source that doesn't go through the Gate. They've been selling pieces of it ever since as their own work, and they're ashamed of that. They weren't exiled for lying. They were exiled for telling people. They went to Sel Arrah once, and they've never told anyone what they saw. It's a green world, with towns, and everyone's still there, in a manner of speaking, on benches and porches, all facing the same way, with the grass grown up around what's left. Ilkin has known all her life. Births in the Deepyards have been falling for a century, and her price for the drydock is the fleet's orphans, so a captain who knows about Sel Arrah has something to bargain with, and something to break. Arc clues: veshti_hazard_code, silent_bearing, lattice_warning. This thread gives the fleet a second road for the last act.

How it might go:
- Finding Tamsin on a patched skiff just past the Deepyards' limit, drawing while they talk: 'That's eleven days from honest.' They want to be believed, and they want one pilot with nerve. Pell is already bouncing in her seat. They show the captain an old spacers' almanac with the Beacon in it, marked with a hazard sign.
- The Deepyards. The Rinse, the portraits, Hearth Rings kept lit and tidy with hardly anyone in them. Ilkin, regal and rude: 'Cargo can wait outside. Meridian may come in.' The route plate hangs in her hall as the family's oldest treasure, and it's the same as Tamsin's chart. Ask her, in front of her family, and watch nobody breathe.
- Sel Arrah. It's a long way off any map, along the first of the old buoys. A green world, with an open sky that's hard on anyone who has been in space for months. Towns, dinner half made and a shop left open. Everyone is still there, facing the same way, and the compass agrees.
- Bringing the news home. Tamsin reads the old family names from the town registers in the Deepyards' great hall, through the front door they've always wanted to come in by. Ilkin's hands don't shake. Her whole family is watching her.
- Drydock or truth? A public reckoning could cost the fleet the only refit it's going to get. Private silence buys the Meridian a new keel, and Tamsin's exile goes on. The chart is the one thing everyone needs.

Ways through:
- Navigator: follow Tamsin's plates through the Ghostlight and out along the buoys (navigation, piloting). Pell will have to fly it for real, later, with the whole fleet behind her.
- Talker: negotiate between Ilkin and Tamsin (persuasion, commerce). Family matters to the Families, and everyone here has an ancestor on a bench somewhere. Madame Oriel bought Tamsin's first chart at full price, and she has her own old score with Ilkin.
- Explorer: walk Sel Arrah (survival, awareness). Work out how it happened from what people were doing. An open sky is hard on Veshti pilots, and it's hard in other ways on everyone.
- Scholar: read the old almanacs and town records (lore). The Families once kept a shift of something, and every family's genealogy has a gap at the same generation.
- Hacker: crack the Deepyards' sealed records (hacking), the birth rolls included, and get what Ilkin has been hiding, with or without Tamsin's help.

Where it can end up:
tamsin_brought_home_open: If Tamsin is brought home in the open, the Families convulse. Ilkin steps down or hardens, and the refit is delayed or becomes a gift. Tamsin joins the crew, and the Thin Road is open.
handed_over_quietly: If they're handed over quietly, the Meridian gets a refit and a stronger hull. Tamsin is confined and their charts are sealed unless someone gets them back. The captain has sold out the first person who got it right.
truth_out: If the truth is out, Veshti reactions range from denial to an ancient grief. Some of the Families will bring ships to the Harbor because it's their turn to make it right. Others will never speak to anyone again.
captain_goes_to_sel_arrah: If the captain goes to Sel Arrah, the trip costs stress, and they'll know exactly what the Quiet leads to, long before the rest of the fleet does.
