# Sold Twice
kind: job
places: graveyard
people: elsbeth, rusk, tarrow, seven
factions: guild, fleet

The lead:
Two dive crews at the Graveyard are shooting at each other over a dead ore hauler, and both of them hold valid Guild contracts for it, in Elsbeth Crane's own neat hand. Each one offers you a third of what's aboard if you settle it their way, and what's aboard is enough hull plate to patch half your fleet.

For anyone who isn't the captain:
Two dive crews at the Graveyard are shooting at each other over a dead ore hauler, and both of them hold valid Guild contracts for it, in Elsbeth Crane's own neat hand. There's enough hull plate aboard to patch half a fleet, and each of them is hiring.

What's really going on:
Elsbeth Crane has never made a mistake on a contract in her life. She sold the hauler Stubborn Margin twice on purpose. Under Guild law, a disputed wreck can't be opened by anyone until the dispute is settled, so two feuding crews make a very cheap fence, and they'll keep everyone else away too. Inside the hauler's forward hold is the reason: during the last battle a Choir Hunter boarded the Stubborn Margin, and her crew killed it with a cargo crane before they died. It's intact. It might be the only whole Hunter anyone has ever had access to, its core still humming. Two divers who went in came out humming too, and they haven't stopped. Crane doesn't want the Hegemony, the Syndicate or anyone else to get their hands on this until she's worked out what it's worth and how it could go wrong, and that's the one thing she hasn't been able to write a contract for. The hull plate is real and the fleet badly needs it. So getting the plate means ending the dispute, and that opens the hold.

How it might go:
- Both crews, on an open channel, insulting each other's mothers in great detail. Their leaders, Hanne Drago and the Okafor brothers, are both reasonable people who are both completely sure.
- Crane's office. She shows the captain both contracts, and neither has a flaw. She'd be very grateful if the matter took a long time to settle. Rusk, somewhere in the background, says: "She never sold a thing twice. Ask yourself why."
- A humming diver, in a Guild sickbay, sits happily tapping out a rhythm on a tin plate. He doesn't know he's doing it. Someone on the Meridian's crew who's heard the song goes pale at the recording.
- The hold. Plate stacked to the ceiling, a crane arm bent and fused, and something silver pinned under it. Its head turns, a little, as the captain's light passes.
- Now everyone wants it. Lt. Tarrow wants it destroyed, Mercy Seven asks very quietly to be allowed to listen to it, and the crews want their plate. Crane wants to know who else the captain has told.

Ways through:
- Settle the dispute: commerce and persuasion to split the salvage so that neither crew loses face, and lore to read how the contracts really work.
- Break in: void_ops, stealth and hacking to cut into the hull while everyone's busy, and see what the fence is for.
- End the shooting: tactics and intimidation to get between the crews and make it stop, or leadership to give both a better offer, working for the fleet.

Checks that fit:
- commerce:moderate to find a split that both crews will sign
- lore:moderate to see that the double sale was deliberate
- void_ops:hard to get into the hauler without tripping anyone's alarm
- tactics:moderate to read the firing lines and find the safe path through
- awareness:easy to notice that nobody who went into the forward hold wants to go back
- science:hard to work out what's safe to do with an intact Hunter

If it comes to a fight:
- wreck_diver from either crew if the captain picks a side too loudly. scrap_rat in the hauler's lower decks. If the Hunter is disturbed, a choir_drone could stir, and a sealed hatch is the best way to deal with it.

Where it can end up:
plate_comes_home_hunter_stays_sealed: If the plate comes home and the Hunter stays sealed, the fleet's hulls are in better shape, the crews have been paid and Crane owes the captain, and the captain knows what's sitting in that hold.
hunter_destroyed: If the Hunter is destroyed, Lt. Tarrow is deeply satisfied, the hummers stop within a week, and some rare knowledge about the Choir is gone for good.
core_comes_back_to_meridian: If the core comes back to the Meridian, the captain gets a choir_core and all the trouble that comes with it. The crew splits over this, and it's possible that something can now hear the ship.
syndicate_hegemony_learns: If the Syndicate or the Hegemony learns of it, Crane's fence is suddenly very thin, and everyone in the Graveyard is about to have a very bad month.
