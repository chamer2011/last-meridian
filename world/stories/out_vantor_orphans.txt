# The Children's Offer
kind: dilemma
places: vantor, haven
people: corvane, naomi, aduro, halm
factions: hegemony, fleet

The lead:
Legate Corvane has a beautiful offer, and for once he isn't hiding the catch. The Hegemony will take every orphan in your fleet, nine hundred and twelve children, and give them beds, teachers and three meals a day at the Vantor Academy. He'd love you to inspect it. You won't find anything wrong.

For anyone who isn't the captain:
Legate Corvane has a beautiful offer for the fleet, and for once he isn't hiding the catch. The Hegemony will take every orphan in it, nine hundred and twelve children, and give them beds, teachers and three meals a day at the Vantor Academy. Anyone can go and inspect it. Nobody has found anything wrong.

What's really going on:
He's telling the truth. The Academy is clean, warm and kind, the food is real, and the teachers love their work. The catch is in plain sight on page two: the children become Hegemony citizens, they're tested gently for Resonance, and at sixteen they're conscripted like everyone else. Dr. Aduro's numbers say that about forty of those children won't make it through the next three months on fleet rations. Parents with living children are already begging the captain to let theirs be counted as orphans. The tour guide is a seventeen-year-old graduate, taken from a rebel colony at six, who says it was the best thing that ever happened to her, and she means it. The moment the player won't expect: Naomi Cass, the Meridian's twelve-year-old stowaway, has already been to look, in a crawlspace on Corvane's own shuttle, and she comes to the captain with a slate. "You didn't ask them. So I did." She's held a vote in every hold on every ship. Six hundred and twelve want to go. Then she points at line four hundred and six of Corvane's list, which is her own name, and which she has crossed out in pencil. "That's a mistake. My dad's on the next shuttle." He isn't on any ship in the fleet. Odessa Brandt's true book says so, and everyone who knows has decided it's someone else's job to tell her. Corvane's clerks work from good records. Yardmother Ilkin Ao-Vash of the Deepyards also wants two hundred of these children, for reasons of her own, so the captain may find that the fleet's orphans are the most sought-after cargo in known space.

How it might go:
- The tour of the Academy. The captain should get every chance to find a flaw. Children laugh, a teacher knows every name, and there's a garden. The guide is honest about conscription, and proud.
- Back in the fleet, Speaker Halm calls it a miracle that can't be refused, and Dr. Aduro quietly gives the captain a number. A mother comes to ask how she could arrange for her son to become an orphan.
- Naomi arrives with her slate, with the results, ship by ship, in a child's careful handwriting. Then she asks the captain to fix the error on line four hundred and six, and waits, looking straight at them.
- Negotiating with Corvane, who's in a very good mood. Everything but the citizenship is on the table, and he seems to have been ready for each thing the captain asks.
- The shuttles are fuelled. Someone has to walk down the line of children and tell them what's been decided.

Ways through:
- Investigate: awareness, science and medicine to look for what's wrong with the Academy, and learn exactly how the Resonance tests work and which children would fail them.
- Bargain: commerce, persuasion and lore for letters home, visits and an exemption for the youngest, or a way out at sixteen.
- Refuse and answer it: leadership and survival to make the fleet feed its children itself, by finding food somewhere else or by asking every adult to eat less, and hold the fleet together when they find out.

Checks that fit:
- awareness:moderate to see what the guide has been told not to mention
- medicine:easy to confirm the children really are healthy here
- commerce:hard to get a real exit clause out of Corvane
- deception:hard to falsify the Resonance tests for the children who'd fail them
- leadership:very_hard to ask thirty thousand hungry people to go hungrier for someone else's children
- composure:hard to tell Naomi Cass the truth about line four hundred and six, or to look at her and not tell her

Where it can end up:
all_go: If all of them go, nine hundred and twelve children are safe and well fed, and the fleet's morale splits down the middle. The Hegemony now has the fleet's hostages in everything but name, and in a few years they'll be soldiers.
only_those_who_voted_to_go: If only those who voted to go are sent, the children themselves split, friends and siblings are separated, and the captain has made a twelve-year-old's election into fleet law.
captain_refuses: If the captain refuses, the fleet keeps its children, and over the next months Dr. Aduro brings the captain a short list of names. Halm uses this. So does Corvane, gently, every time they meet.
captain_wins_exit_clause: If the captain wins an exit clause, some graduates will come home at sixteen, in Hegemony uniform, for reasons the captain won't be able to tell at the time.
whatever_happens_to_children: Whatever happens to the children, the captain now knows what's on line four hundred and six. If they tell Naomi, she doesn't speak to anyone for two days, and then she goes back to work. If they don't, she finds out anyway, and that's when they lose her.

Rewards:
Rations freed up across the fleet if the children go, and Naomi's trust if she's taken seriously. A warm note in Corvane's own hand and a modest rise in hegemony standing if the deal is signed.
