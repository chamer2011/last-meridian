# Three Prices
kind: dilemma
places: cinder, kessara, haven
people: quill, corvane, ulla, marrow, odessa
factions: drift, hegemony, fleet

The lead:
Dunn Marrow has stopped swearing about the fuel, and that's when you know it's bad. There are three places to get more, and you've now heard all three prices. Mother Quill wants forty-one favors. Legate Corvane wants one small list. And Shift Boss Ulla Greaves at Cinder Reach wants you to help her win a war.

What's really going on:
Quill's price is forty-one blank markers, one signed by the captain of each ship, each good for one favor, to be named later. They look harmless, and she'll sell them on to anyone: one of them could order a ship to change course, hand over a passenger or stand aside. Corvane's price is a census, nothing more: name, age, skills and any unusual medical sensitivity, which is how the Ministry tests for Resonance. His tankers arrive free of charge, with Hegemony crews, very good sensors and a Hegemony transponder fitted to every tank. Ulla's price is that the fleet's people stand beside her strikers when the company's men come, which means taking a side in somebody else's fight, and Rennick Vale would pay the same fuel to have the fleet stand on the other side. The surprise is in Odessa Brandt's true books, or Talia Sen's gossip, or a sharp look at any delivery note: it's all the same fuel. Everything comes from Cinder Reach. Quill's depot is running down its stockpile, the Hegemony's tankers buy from Quill, and the strike has tripled the price. Quill's money, through three shell charities, is what's keeping the strike kitchen open, and Ulla Greaves doesn't know it. Madame Oriel at the Gilded Wheel also holds a marker on Quill's depot, won in one hand thirty years ago and never called, which a very bold captain might try to buy.

How it might go:
- Three meetings. Quill feeds the captain and worries about them. Corvane is reasonable and already has the census form printed. Ulla is at the Gate on Shift Row, shouting, and she's the only one who says plainly what she wants.
- The fleet argues. Speaker Halm wants the Hegemony's tankers, a dozen small captains refuse to sign anything for Quill, and a miner's cousin on the Sundial is collecting for the strike fund.
- Odessa pulls out the books. The captain sees the shape of it: one source, three prices, and everyone's living off a strike that's starving six thousand people.
- Fuel hits ten days, and something has to be signed. The GM should push: a ship goes dark to save its reserves, and Marrow starts turning off the Meridian's heating, one deck at a time.
- A choice, or a clever mixture, and the bill that comes with it. Whichever deal it is, the captain should feel it follow them for the rest of the game.

Ways through:
- Haggle and combine: commerce and persuasion to split the need among suppliers, so that no one holds all the cards, and get the terms down to something survivable.
- Expose the game: streetwise, hacking and lore to prove where the fuel really comes from and who's funding what, then use it to break the price.
- Need less: engineering and navigation to run the fleet on less, with slow burns and shared tows, plus leadership to make cold, slow, frightened people accept it.

Checks that fit:
- commerce:hard to get the marker count below forty-one
- streetwise:moderate to learn who's really funding the strike
- engineering:hard to cut the fleet's fuel burn by a fifth
- navigation:moderate to plot a slower, cheaper route
- persuasion:moderate to get Corvane to leave the medical question off the form
- leadership:hard to make the fleet accept any of these prices

If it comes to a fight:
- strikebreaker or drift_raider only if the argument spills over at Cinder Reach or a depot. There's no fight in this that the captain has to win.

Where it can end up:
captain_signs_quills_markers: If the captain signs Quill's markers, the fleet has fuel and owes it to a woman who collects. The GM should cash one in every so often, and one should turn up in very strange hands.
captain_gives_corvane_census: If the captain gives Corvane the census, fuel flows. Within weeks, polite people are visiting the ships on the list of unusual medical sensitivities, and Vael has a very good month.
captain_backs_strike: If the captain backs the strike, the fleet makes real friends on Cinder Reach and real enemies in the Syndicate, and leads straight into the fight over the reactor.
price_broken_by_exposing_game: If the price is broken by exposing the game, Quill sends a card and raises her price on something else, and Ulla Greaves learns who's been paying for her strike. That starts another kind of fight.

If nobody steps in:
after 23 days: Mother Quill's markers, Legate Corvane's list and Ulla Greaves's war: the whole fleet has heard the three prices for its fuel, and everyone's got an opinion.
  lead: The whole fleet's heard the three prices for its fuel by now, and everyone's got an opinion about which one you should pay. Chief Marrow hasn't got an opinion. He's got a number, and it's getting smaller.
  fleet: morale -1

Rewards:
Fuel, through fuel_cell or refined_fuel items, in the amount the deal allows, and completion of the fleet's fuel quest. Standing changes with drift, hegemony and the strikers, depending on who the captain chose.
