# The Other Meridian
kind: exploration
places: graveyard
people: lira, marrow, okonkwo, garrick
factions: fleet, remnant, guild

The lead:
Ensign Lira Vey's skin goes white before she says a word. Deep in the Graveyard her sensors have found a burned-out Concord frigate with your ship's registry on its transponder, your ship's name on its hull, and the same crooked patch on the same strut that Dunn Marrow welded on last spring.

For anyone who isn't the captain:
Divers deep in the Graveyard have found a burned-out Concord frigate with the Meridian's registry on its transponder, her name on its hull, and the same crooked patch on the same strut that Chief Marrow welded on last spring.

What's really going on:
In the last week of the Concord, the Navy ran a shell game. The Choir was hunting flagships by their codes, so the identity of the CFS Meridian was copied onto a sister frigate, the CFS Halloway, right down to the scars, to draw the hunters away from the evacuations. The Halloway flew into the last battle under the Meridian's name, with a hundred and eighty crew, and died there. The Choir found the decoy, and that's why the real Meridian lived long enough for the survivors to rally around her. The Navy listed the Halloway's crew as deserters to protect the secret, and their families are in the fleet right now, treated as the kin of cowards, and in some cases short-rationed for it. Dunn Marrow recognizes the weld as his own hand: he was told it was a training mock-up. The order was signed by Admiral Amara Okonkwo-Reyes. On the Halloway's bridge the last log has a message from her captain, Jorun Eide, addressed to "whoever is flying the real one". She doesn't ask for anything. She just hopes it was worth it, and she wants the names read out.

How it might go:
- Going aboard. Everything is the wrong kind of familiar: the same corridors and deck numbers, and the same scuffed handrail outside the captain's cabin. The captain's own cabin door has someone else's name on it.
- Marrow arrives on a line, looking at the patch. He doesn't swear, and that's when everyone gets scared. Then he does swear, for several minutes, very quietly.
- The bridge, and Captain Eide's message. The names of the crew follow, one after another, and some of them are names the captain knows from a list of people the fleet has been spitting on.
- A Guild claim-jumper crew turns up, or word leaks, or Garrick Fenn picks up something on a very unlicensed channel. The secret has hours left.
- Back on the Meridian. A woman named Ottilie Brandvold, whose son was the Halloway's navigator and whose ration card has been stamped D for six months, is waiting in the queue outside the mess. What does the captain say to her?

Ways through:
- Search the wreck: void_ops and science to get through dead compartments and pull the log before it degrades, and awareness to read what the damage says about how she died.
- Trace the order: lore and hacking through the Halloway's sealed files to find who ordered this, and why it was buried.
- Face the living: leadership and composure when telling Marrow, the crew, the families or the fleet, and persuasion to decide together what the Meridian's legend is now.

Checks that fit:
- void_ops:moderate to move through a wreck that's still shifting
- science:moderate to prove the transponder was copied, and when
- hacking:hard to open the Halloway's sealed orders
- composure:moderate to read the names on that bridge
- leadership:very_hard to tell thirty thousand people that their miracle had a price
- lore:hard to find the order's signature in the Navy's old codes

If it comes to a fight:
- scrap_rat in the lower decks and wreck_diver if a crew of claim-jumpers arrives. Filing a quick claim with Factor Crane, or sharing the salvage, is as good as a gun.

Where it can end up:
captain_tells_fleet: If the captain tells the fleet, the Halloway's families are cleared and honored. The fleet's belief that the Meridian can't be killed takes a hit, but some people love the real story more. Morale falls, then slowly rises.
captain_keeps_quiet_clears_families: If the captain keeps it quiet and clears the families some other way, they're helped and they never learn why, and Marrow knows the captain made that choice. It's a very heavy secret if it ever comes out.
kept_entirely_hidden: If it's kept entirely hidden, nothing changes. Ottilie Brandvold goes on standing in that queue, and Garrick Fenn goes on digging.
signature_traced: If the signature is traced, the captain has something real to say to Admiral Okonkwo-Reyes when they meet: she's the one who decided which Meridian lived.
