HOW TO WRITE A PLACE
====================

Every place the fleet can reach is one file in this folder, named by its id: haven.txt is Haven Station, and the
stories, the people and the star map name it by that id. The Story board in the game (The story, then Story board,
then Places) shows and changes the same files, and npm run check-story reads them all and says what's wrong, with
the file and the line. What a place looks like when the player walks around in it is in world/maps.

A file starts with the name, then a few lines about it, then parts under headings that end with a colon:

    # Haven Station
    also called: Haven
    faction: drift                      (who holds it: an id from world/factions.json, or none)
    market: medkit, stim, energy_cell   (what's for sale there: ids from world/items.json; leave it empty for no market)
    the fleet is here: no
    a place to rest: no
    dangerous: no
    hidden: no                          (not on the star map until the story shows it)
    people: sable, tharel, corvane      (who's there, by id)
    enemies: dock_thug, syndicate_collector     (who might start a fight, by id from world/enemies)
    items: data_chip, forged_papers     (what might turn up, by id)

    In a line:
    A neutral trade hub where every faction drinks in the same bar and pretends not to notice.

    The feel of it:
    Frying synth-fat, ozone and old beer, under lighting that was meant to be warm.

    Districts:
    The Neutral Ground: Jun Sable's bar, where weapons go in a locked rack by the door.
    Consulate Row: The Hegemony's carpet, and the only quiet corridor on the station.

    Complications:
    - Mott Jessup has quietly raised your docking fee.

    Secrets:
    - Mother Quill's Syndicate holds the mortgage on Haven's docks.

    Encounters:
    - Jun Sable slides you a drink you didn't order and a bill for it that's much too high.

    Rumors:
    - Word at the Neutral Ground is that Sable has never once forgotten a tab.

In a line is what the player reads on the star map. The feel of it, the districts, the complications and the
secrets are for the game master, and the AI runs the place from them. Encounters are what can happen when the
player arrives, and rumors are what people say, which the Leads menu shows: one line each, starting with - and a
space. A part that has nothing in it can be left out.
