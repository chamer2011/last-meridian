HOW TO WRITE AN ITEM
====================

Everything the player can carry, buy or be given is one file in this folder, named by its id: service_rifle.txt is
the Service Rifle, and places name what they sell by that id. The Story board in the game (The story, then Story
board, then Items) shows and changes the same files, and npm run check-story reads them all and says what's wrong,
with the file and the line.

    # Service Rifle
    order: 23                           (where it comes in lists; leave it out to come after the rest, by name)
    type: weapon                        (weapon, armor, consumable, gadget or story)
    price: 250
    damage: 5 to 9                      (weapons)
    stat: perception                    (weapons: the attribute that adds to the attack)
    skill: firearms                     (weapons: the skill that adds to the attack)
    defense: 2                          (armor)
    restricted: yes                     (not for sale to just anyone; leave it out if not)
    effect: hp 10, energy 5             (consumables: what using it does; hp, energy, hull or fuel)

    In a line:
    The old navy's standard long gun, easy to find now that so few are left to carry one.

    Effects:
    in a setting: vacuum 1              (while it's worn or carried: easier or harder in a setting)
    skills: hacking 1                   (a bonus or penalty to a skill)

In a line is what the player reads in the inventory and the market. A part that has nothing in it can be left out.
