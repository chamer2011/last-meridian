In a line:
Gentle, patient and the most persuasive voice the Quiet has in the fleet. People who go to argue with him tend to come back rested.

Appearance:
A soft-faced, sandy man with green-stained fingers and a very patient way of standing. He usually has a seedling tray with him, and he'll give you whichever seedling you look at.

Personality:
Kind all the way down. He has sat with more of the dying than anyone but the doctors, and he's never raised his voice or asked anyone for anything. He would never hurt a living soul, and he may be about to get thirty thousand of them killed.

Voice:
Soft and slow, and he agrees with you first: "Yes. Yes, that's true." Then he goes on as if you hadn't argued, though he'll honestly concede a good point. Opens nearly every conversation with "You must be tired", asks how you've been sleeping, and doesn't mention the Quiet unless you do. Never says "dead", "killed" or "taken". People are "kept": "Your sister's been kept." Hands your own words back to you a few minutes later, and lets silences run until you fill them.

Goals:
He wants everyone to stop being afraid, and he wants to be kept alongside his daughter Anneli. He'd like to bring as many people as he can with him, gently, and that includes the captain.

Secrets:
During the Fall, a Choir mimic came to his door with his daughter Anneli's face, and it sang the song he'd made up to get her to sleep. No one else ever knew that song. That's his whole faith, and it can't be argued with. He retuned the Drowned Deck's pumps by hand to the pitch of the song, and people sleep there as they sleep nowhere else. Hidden in the Sundial's irrigation controllers is a small transmitter he calls the lantern. It sends out the names of people who've asked to be kept, so the Choir will know to come for them. He thinks only the Choir can hear it. Iven Sorel can, and so can anything else, and it could lead anyone to the fleet. His people collect Dr. Adeyemi's "transfers" on a drift-boat. A few of his inner circle want to open the door and call the Choir in, and he doesn't know. Yara Solune brought him into the Quiet at Haven. He used to set out chairs for Father Roe's services, he still loves him, and he has been leaving sacks of Sundial flour outside his galley.
