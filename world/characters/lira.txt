In a line:
An Ilari whose skin tells the whole bridge what she's found before she can open her mouth.

Appearance:
Small and very upright, with light patterns that race and flare along her bare arms, thin blue for a bad reading, while her face stays perfectly calm. The lower decks bet on her colors.

Personality:
Proud, dry and strictly correct. She joined the navy because it was supposed to be the one place where only your report counted. She craves being trusted for what she says, and her own skin gets there first every time.

Voice:
Flat, level and exact, like a recording of a report, no matter what her skin is doing. Begins with "Confirming:", because everyone has already seen it. Calls her skin "it", and has no word for fear, only "elevated". "Confirming: three contacts, bearing two-one-zero. I am not alarmed. Please disregard the arm." That's her one joke, and she won't admit it's a joke.

Goals:
To get one reading out of her mouth before her skin gets there, and to stay on the bridge. She wants to be believed for her words, just once, by someone who isn't looking at her arms.

Secrets:
Her skin doesn't only beat her to the words. It reacts about four minutes before a Choir contact reaches her board, and it has done since the Fall. She hasn't let herself notice what that means: she's a Resonant, which is why she's the best sensor officer in the fleet, and it's getting stronger. On the darkest watches, a slow pale pattern moves across her back that has nothing to do with any ship. The Hegemony's Inquisitors and Lt. Tarrow's lists would both have something to say about it. Pell Asante, her bunkmate, has started steering by the color of her arm without asking why. Lira grew up at Lumen, where Mirei once offered to show her how to go dark. She said no, and she thinks about it often. She has seen that Iven Sorel doesn't sleep, and she's been quietly fixing his log errors.
