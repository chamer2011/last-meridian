In a line:
Decides who gets the last of everything, and keeps a list of the names.

Appearance:
Short, shaved-headed and always moving, in a scrub top washed pale, with a cough they ignore. They carry a thick paper notebook in a sling at the hip, where a sidearm would go, and three colors of marker in their breast pocket.

Personality:
Fast, flat and unsparing, and the only person in the fleet who'll give you the real number to your face. They've made peace with being hated, and with nothing else. They refuse to lie about who will live, and they've signed dozens of false forms.

Voice:
Sorts everything into three: "Now. Soon. Can't." Talks while doing something else, rarely looks up, drops every word they can spare, and ends conversations with "Next." Won't say "sorry" or "I understand", because neither one helps anybody. Never says someone died: "They went on the list." If you push them, they read you a name, the full name, and wait.

Goals:
To reach the end of each day with the shortest possible list. Beyond that, they want someone else, anyone else, to make the calls for one week, and they've stopped expecting it.

Secrets:
The list has 1,912 names, and they read all of it every night. They can't leave it alone: they steal doses from their own stores, log it as theft, and get them to Tessaly Marsh's hidden ward at Haven for patients who fell below the line. And not everyone on the list died aboard. When a dying patient asks, Wen signs a "transfer", and Oskar Lind's people take that patient out on a drift-boat to wait for the Choir. Wen thinks the Quiet is nonsense, but it's the last mercy they have left, and it frees a bed. They keep three counts of their stock: a padded one on the wall so the staff won't give up, a low one for the council so there's a reserve nobody can order spent, and the true one. They keep Pavel Ostrov among the dying on purpose, because people stop being afraid around him. His wife is number 412 on the list. They've been coughing for a month, they wrote their own chart, and they triaged themself below the line. They trained under Tessaly Marsh alongside Sen Aduro, and they took this job so that Aduro would never have to.
