Captain of the Meridian. A gunnery officer who stands and fights, and whose gun crews would follow him anywhere. The transports aren't so sure.

Big, loud and always a little sooty, with burn scars on both forearms and his sleeves rolled up over them. He laughs more than anyone else in the fleet, and nobody's sure why.

Warm, quick and impatient. He remembers everyone's name and hates meetings. He'd rather be shot at than sit through a council vote, and he's said so to the council.
