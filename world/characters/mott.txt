In a line:
Knows every ship's real manifest, and can be persuaded in several currencies.

Appearance:
A large, damp, cheerful man with a slate under each arm and a mustache he has clearly given up on. His office is a glass box over the main dock, and he's always eating something someone has just given him.

Personality:
Jolly, shameless and crooked as an old pipe. He'll sell anything except a refugee ship's air and water allotment, which he has never once shorted, and he'll deny that too. He has a large, loud family on the station, and he's been quietly terrified since the Fall.

Voice:
Talks about himself as two people. "The office" is the Dockmaster, who can't possibly do that. "Mott" is a friend of yours who might. "The office says that berth's closed. Mott says, what've you brought him?" Genial, quick and sweaty, and never gives a flat yes or a flat no, only "Could be, could be." His currencies are credits, favors, a look at someone else's manifest, and real coffee.

Goals:
Buy guaranteed berths on a fleet ship for his mother, his late wife's sisters and his three daughters before Haven gets hit. He'd pay nearly anything, and the person he'd most like to pay is the captain.

Secrets:
Every month, unmarked Hegemony ships refuel at his back dock, going out past the border full and coming back empty. He took the money without asking. Then one night he looked through a hatch and saw people. He has kept a private copy of every manifest since, and told no one, because he can't think who'd be safe to tell. He pays a tithe to Mother Quill and another to Jun Sable, and he's on Legate Corvane's books as well. He has been selling Odessa Brandt off-book stores for years. Their letters are all in cargo codes, and on his side they've become something he won't name: "One tin coffee, real, no charge." Inspector Moor has started asking about his fuel logs.
