In a line:
Polite, quick and very good at his job, and he looks like he hasn't slept in weeks.

Appearance:
A long, thin young man with a headset he forgets to take off, one ear rubbed raw from it, shadows under his eyes like thumbprints and a tremor in his hands.

Personality:
Meticulous and kind, with a sense of humor worn down to a thread. He'd report anyone else for what he's doing. He's frightened of what he hears, and he's more frightened of anyone making him stop listening.

Voice:
Speaks low, as if someone's asleep nearby. Radio habits have leaked into his talk: "Say again?", "Copy", and on bad nights a quiet "over" at the end of a sentence. Sometimes he stops mid-sentence, says "Hold on", listens to something you can't hear, then carries on from the exact next word. He'll say "noise", but never "nothing" and never "silence".

Goals:
To hear his sister say one thing she's never said before, so he'll know whether it's really her. After that, to sleep.

Secrets:
Every night on a dead band, under the static, he hears his sister Anja reading out a crew list. Anja was a comms tech on the CFS Tarn, listed as lost with all hands. The list is the Tarn's roster, in order, with one name left out: Ilya Voss. He looked it up, and he hasn't dared ask the Commander why. And every signaller has a fist, a rhythm as personal as handwriting: the hand keying the Navy overlay on the Beacon is hers. He hasn't reported any of it. Either he's insane, or they'll take him off the board, and that's the only place he can still hear her. He hasn't slept in nineteen days. Under her, other voices read other names, and he has filled eleven paper notebooks with them, in tiny careful letters. They're the Concord's dead, and his husband Tomas is in notebook four, but twenty-three of them are alive in this fleet, including, somehow, Commander Voss. Some nights, not quite knowing he's doing it, he patches a transmitter into a spare antenna and reads the notebooks out in Garrick Fenn's cadence, and thousands stay up to listen. He knows where Fenn's real transmitter is, too, and he hasn't reported that either.
