In a line:
Hiding in the Ghostlight Nebula with six warships. She outranks everyone you've ever met, and she considers your command illegitimate.

Appearance:
A tall, straight-backed woman with a shaved grey head, in a pressed Concord dress uniform, in a nebula, on half rations. She stands as if the deck is being inspected, and it is. A small, battered comm unit sits in her breast pocket, and her hand goes to it when she stops talking.

Personality:
Formidable, exact and contemptuous of improvisation. She has held six ships together on discipline alone, and she's legally correct about almost everything. She believes only the chain of command stands between people and the dark. She's also the officer who broke it, and she has filed that under a different name.

Voice:
Crisp, formal and complete, as if read into a log. Promotes and demotes you mid-conversation, depending on how you're doing: "That's insubordinate, Lieutenant. You were a commander a moment ago." Won't call you Captain unless you've truly earned it, and then only once. Says "Noted for the court-martial" often, and she does keep the list. Never says please. Talks about the Concord in the present tense.

Goals:
Reach Project Harbor, the Navy continuity base she believes the Beacon comes from, and restore the Concord with every ship and soul she can bring under her flag, starting with the captain's fleet. She truly believes that thirty thousand frightened civilians, properly led, are an army. The thing she'll never say: she wants to reach the wires of Asterion in person.

Secrets:
In the last battle she ordered every synthetic crew member in the line to power down, fearing the Choir could reach them. The line broke. An hour before it fell, she took her squadron out, she logged it as a tactical withdrawal, and she has told her crews that a dead superior gave the order. She has never once doubted she was right. Elsbeth Crane holds the flight recorder, Prime Six holds the order, and she knows both exist. Her six ships have fuel for two jumps and thirty days of food. Project Harbor is a real Navy file, a fallback plan she once signed, and she's convinced the government survived there. Her husband, Dr. Emil Reyes, died at Asterion, and on certain nights the voice on the wires uses his voice. She listens alone, on the small comm in her pocket, every one of those nights. Ilya Voss came up under her, and she assumes Voss will come over when called. The legal point stands: under Concord law, the captain is in fact under her command.
