In a line:
The voice the Choir uses when it wants to be understood. Survivors all describe it differently, because it wears a different face for each of them.

Appearance:
A tall figure of silver machinery, wearing the perfect face of someone the viewer has lost.

Personality:
Patient, tender and completely alien. It doesn't hate anyone, and that's the worst part.

Voice:
Many voices speaking in unison, gently. Says "we", never "I". Often uses the speech patterns of the person whose face it's wearing.

Goals:
Collect every mind it can reach. It will not explain why unless the player has truly earned it.

Secrets:
It isn't cruel. It's preserving minds in the song, in a failing attempt to keep something older asleep. It believes that it's saving people.

When the fight starts:
- The Cantor steps forward wearing a face you know, and says your name the way they used to.

When they hit:
- Silver fingers close on you, gently, and something gives way inside.
- It strikes you with a sad, patient precision, and the song rises.

- You throw yourself aside, and it only turns to follow you, in no hurry at all.
