== truth
You say it. Two signals, locked together. On top, a Concord Navy recall, months old, plain: safe harbor, all vessels come home. Underneath, something older than anyone can say, that nobody can read, and that the Navy voice is sitting on top of like a lid.

The room splits down the middle so fast you can hear it: the half that heard "Navy" and the half that heard "nobody can read". Fenn's recorder is pointed at your face. Voss has looked at you, which she doesn't do in rooms.

== carry_pass
You stand up, and you don't raise your voice, because the Meridian's bridge taught you that the quiet ones get listened to. You tell them what a fleet is for. You tell them that a recall is a recall, and that the people who sent it don't care what's under it, and that neither do you, until you've got everyone home. You tell them nobody's going to lie to them in this room, ever, and that's why they're being told now.

The room comes back. Not all at once, and not all of it, but the half that heard "nobody can read" has started hearing "home".

== send_tharel
Tharel comes across on the Haven shuttle in a coat that isn't his, the first time he's been off the station in nine days, and he stands at the head of the table beside you because nobody thought to give him a chair, and he tells them, in the plainest words anyone's heard him use, about the Navy layer. Fleet-recall format. Months old. Someone in uniform, calling the fleet home.

He doesn't say the word lattice. He doesn't say what's under it. He says that he's an old man and a cautious one, and sits down on the bench a clerk gives up.
