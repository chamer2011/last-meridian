== morning
The signal is on every band this morning, and nobody who outranks you will say what it means. Where you stand, the work doesn't care.

Whatever this means for the fleet is happening a long way over your head. For now there's the day in front of you: the people here, the work, and whatever they've heard.

NOTE: [STORY] This life's own opening hasn't been written yet. You can walk around, work, talk to people about what they've heard, and watch the world go on.

> Get on with the day -> day

== day
You get on with it. The signal keeps going, six seconds at a time, under everything else.
END
