== asked_chief
@Chief Engineer Dunn Marrow: "A signal." He says it the way he'd say a rat. "On every band, since the middle watch. The bridge thinks it's a harbor. The bridge thinks a lot of things. What I know is that every receiver on this ship is pulling power to listen to it, and I'm the one who finds the power." He goes back to the line. "If it's a harbor, it'll have a dock. If it's got a dock, it'll have parts. That's as far as my opinion goes."

== gossip
The gossip comes down the ducts before it comes down the ladder. Chief Hask is in the hatchway with his pocket scale out, weighing nothing, listening. A girl of about twelve goes past overhead inside the trunking, which isn't possible, and says "technically it's a formal notice" to nobody. The mess hands say it's the Navy. The mess hands said that about the last three things.

@Chief Tobin Hask: "They'll want to go and look at it." Slow, low. "The chair always does. I'll weigh the order when it comes. It'll come up light. They always do, the first week."

== back_to_work

That's the end of the written opening. You can work a shift, talk to people about what they've heard, and watch the fleet decide where it's going. If it goes to Haven, the Star map will take you across.
