What the player sees:
Forty-one green dots on a scratched glass table, and the course Pell keeps for you drawn through them in chalk, because the table's own line stopped working years ago.

Files:
course.txt: The course as the bridge keeps it: where the fleet is, where it's going, and how many jumps of fuel are in the tanks. Pell updates it when he remembers, which is most days.
fleet_manifest.txt: Forty-one ships, with what each carries, who captains it, and the day it last reported. Half the entries are in Voss's hand, and hers are the ones that are right.

Mail:
From Voss to the captain: The list is on the table. Which ships can't keep up, and the day we stop trying to make them. I'd rather you read it than heard it from Fenn.

Programs:
Run the sensor sweep: Six seconds of nothing, and then the signal, on every band, the way Sorel described it. The table draws it as a line that goes off the edge of the glass.
Log the day's orders: Your orders go into the ship's log, in your own words, where the council can read them if it ever asks. -> * journal: Logged the day's orders at the plot table.
