What the player sees:
A machine that was expensive once, in an office that smells of the water the pipe is leaking. The harbor master's whole trade is in it: who owes, who paid, and who's allowed to forget.

Files:

water.txt: The water contract, and the names of the three people who know what the pipe is actually for.
