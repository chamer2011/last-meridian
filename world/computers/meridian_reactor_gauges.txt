What the player sees:
A bank of gauges older than anyone aboard, with the readings that matter circled in grease pencil: reactor load, coolant, the air plant, and the tanks.

Files:
maintenance.txt: What was fixed, what was patched, and what was written down as fixed and patched. Marrow's hand on most of it, and a few entries in a hand that stops the day of the Fall.
air_plant.txt: The air plant's numbers, by deck. Nine hundred people breathing on a ship built for three hundred, and the plant running at a hundred and forty percent of what it was built for. There's a note in the margin: "it will do this as long as it does this".
