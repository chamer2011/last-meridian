# The sublevel door panel

What the player sees:
A keypad and a very new lock, on a very old door, with a cable as thick as your arm running under it, warm to the touch. Somebody down there is drawing a great deal of power.

Files:
access.log: Opened and closed. One name, over and over, for a year: hollis-nine. Nobody else has been through.
draw.txt: Power drawn through the cable, by the hour. More than this whole level uses. Whatever's below is running something that never sleeps.

- people

Programs:

Read the lock's log: Hollis-Nine comes up at odd hours, and goes down again, and the door is never open more than a minute at a time.
